# Other construction routes

The [active route](ACTIVE.md) retains one intact parent. The following alternatives were recorded for later comparison.

| Approach | Evidence and next question |
|---|---|
| Replace a seven-dimensional middle and free the four outer pieces | A fixed 47-edge middle has 1,108 disjoint partners of the same length. The four nearest unresolved replacements each delete and add three vertices. Test their possible joining sites. [Middle census and exclusions](../../milestones/247-relative-middle-placements/README.md) |
| Use two or more crossings | Prescribe compatible pieces in alternating faces, or use the crossing pattern of the five archived joins outside the congruent-middle family. Retained pieces in the same face must also avoid unwanted adjacencies. [Dimension lifts](../../milestones/188-dimensional-constructions/MATHEMATICAL_NOTE.md) · [Two-crossing frontier](../../milestones/230-two-crossing-frontier/) |
| Reroute one or two windows | Release a contiguous segment and its neighbours, or shorten one window to make room to lengthen a second. With fixed endpoints the length change of a window is even. [Earlier reroutings](../../milestones/195-shortened-reroutings/) · [Coupled crossings](../../milestones/223-coupled-four-crossings/) |
| Open a coil or reconnect several components | Preserve a shorter arc of the 192-vertex coil, change the cuts, or reconnect induced path components. Long retained arcs have already been searched. [Coil opening](../../milestones/243-coil-opening/README.md) · [Published snakepit constructions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15270) |
| Change the starting path | Use independent 190-edge seeds, new eight-dimensional shapes, or partial transition sequences. Direct growth and beam search can generate candidates. Each candidate needs a complete induced-path check. [190-edge parents](../../milestones/244-190-path-construction/README.md) · [Free endpoints](../../milestones/245-free-endpoint-reconstruction/README.md) · [Parent diversity](../../milestones/199-parent-diversity/) |
| Search for symmetric paths | Impose a coordinate isometry or repeated transition pattern on a construction and check all adjacencies across pieces. A proof for a symmetric family leaves general paths open. [Symmetry placements](../../milestones/197-symmetry-placement/) · [Cyclic symmetry](../../milestones/204-cyclic-symmetry/) |
