# When an entire face is fixed as several disjoint paths

In the 131 longest bank snakes the least-used coordinate changes once in 25 cases, twice in 88, three times in 12 and four times in six. Erasing all edges of such a coordinate leaves alternating components in two eight-dimensional faces. For two, three or four crossings, collect the face sides with at least two components. Include every tied choice of least-used axis. This gives 132 indexed face sides and 111 cube-isometry classes of forests. Of the latter, 101 have two components and ten have three. Their vertex counts range from 94 to 99.

**Result.** None of these 111 prescribed forests can be the complete intersection of a 192-edge snake with a face of the nine-cube. The opposite face is completely free. In particular, take any of the 18 sources whose least-used direction occurs three or four times. In any such direction a successful construction must change both face vertex sets, even when the rest of the path is allowed to change freely.

For any fixed forest, the opposite copies of its internal vertices are forbidden because an internal vertex already has degree two. A copied forest endpoint is a crossing. An uncopied forest endpoint is an endpoint of the global path. Each forest component must cross at one or both ends to reach the rest of the snake. Thus if the fixed forest has c nontrivial components and the global snake has k crossings, exactly 2c − k of the two global endpoints lie on the fixed face. In particular k is one of 2c−2, 2c−1, 2c. This identity retains all possible crossing counts in a single formula.

Select exactly 193 minus the forest size in the opposite face and impose the total of two global endpoints. At a crossed root, the crossing itself supplies one of the prescribed incidences. Impose the global cube parity of the two ends and the vertex counts 97 and 96. Exclude selected induced cycles inside the opposite face. The collected 111 initial formulas already give UNSAT with no added connectivity clauses, using 21,241,068 solver conflicts in total. The 192-vertex control for the first case reconstructs a 191-edge path, checked against every pair.
