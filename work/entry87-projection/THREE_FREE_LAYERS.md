# Which layers must move to reach 192 edges?

The public 190-edge bank contains fifteen snakes that visit all four seven-dimensional layers exactly once, in the order A, B, C, D around a square. The four-segment source at row 0 has 49, 48, 47 and 47 vertices. A 192-edge target needs 193 vertices in total. The layer paths can be rebuilt as arbitrary induced Q7 paths.

**Adjacent-pair theorem.** For each of these fifteen labelled four-layer configurations, keeping any two adjacent layer vertex sets fixed prevents a 192-edge snake of the same four-layer order. This covers all 60 source/pair choices and arbitrary compatible endpoint orientations of the frozen sets. In particular a move that changes just two layers sharing a crossing fails if it leaves the other two in their source placements.

For fixed A,B, their one common address forces their join. The other endpoint of B forces the root of C. Grow C one edge at a time in the complement of B apart from that root. At a potential end h outside A, grow D from h in the complement of A and of C, apart from h. This tries every shared C,D crossing exactly where it can occur. Reversing the whole path gives the case of fixed C,D. There are 30 source/orientation cases.

For fixed B,C, their shared address determines the two outer roots. Grow A backwards from the free endpoint of B, avoiding the other addresses in B and the future D root. Grow D from the free endpoint of C, avoiding the other addresses in C and every address in A. Since A,D are adjacent without a path crossing, their projected sets must be disjoint. There are fifteen cases.

For fixed A,D, they are disjoint and neither carries a forced crossing to the other. Choose one of the two endpoints on each for the B and C joins. Grow B from its fixed A root; at each possible shared B,C hinge h grow C to the chosen endpoint of D. Include all four endpoint assignments per source. When the two resultant outer endpoints have the wrong global parity, a 192-edge path is impossible. Of 60 assignments, 22 fail this parity test and 38 are exhausted directly.

The recursion drops neighbours of a former path endpoint as soon as the next step is taken, so every internally grown segment is induced. Its component and bipartition bounds give upper bounds on the remaining possible length. Every layer has at most 51 vertices, and both flexible lengths sum to 193 minus the fixed sizes. Across all three modes the final 105 cases take 1,661,494,804 third-layer states and 129,188,958 last-layer states; 83 terminate by exhaustion and 22 by endpoint parity. Every source was reconstructed at its known 190-edge length. A separate test directly enumerated the induced Q3 layer paths and sampled 18,000 four-layer decisions across all modes and endpoint choices. All 3,069 positive and 14,931 negative cases match the independent direct join. ### A constructive search with one fixed layer

**All six pairs.** The two opposite choices of fixed layers also fail for every source, allowing all four assignments of their free crossing ends. Among 120 indexed assignments, 30 have their previously certified source orientation, 16 have a crossing endpoint already occupied by the other fixed path, and the remaining 74 are excluded by the two independent rooted paths. Together with the adjacent-pair theorem this covers **all 90 choices** of a pair among four layers across the fifteen source placements. A 192-edge snake built in the same labelled four-layer order must change **at least three layer vertex sets** relative to each source. [Endpoint assignment checker](opposite_orientations.py), [first five sources](opposite-orientations-0-5-12000000.json), [middle five](opposite-orientations-5-10-12000000.json), [last five](opposite-orientations-10-15-12000000.json).

Fix A. Keep only a contiguous core of the opposite C from the same source and remove a total of 15 end vertices of C, distributing the removals between its two ends in every way. Grow new induced arms on both ends of that core; allow C to change size up to 51. For every new C, let U be the Q7 addresses in neither A nor C. The middle B is any path in U together with its two crossing addresses, from the chosen endpoint of A to the first endpoint of C. The last D is independently any path starting at the second endpoint of C in U together with that root. The ability to reuse the same elements of U in B and D is essential, since those layers are opposite. The two separate maxima add exactly. [Core generation and independent arm search](one_layer_core.py), [four-layer criterion](FOUR_LAYERS.md).

Across both source orientations, this produced 5,747 indexed opposite-layer paths. In an initial run, 3,625 ranked candidates were tested and 2,995 were excluded at the required length by rigorously computed upper bounds. The search reproduced 190 edges. The residual cases can be tested by increasing the per-arm budget or by certifying the required size in each rooted residual graph. Increasing the number of removed end vertices explores new seven-dimensional shapes. This family lets B, C and D change together while holding A fixed.

An expanded run considered **all 5,747** candidates. The bounds exclude **5,365**, leaving **382 indexed cases**. For each case compare the sum of the two exact rooted-arm maxima to 193−|A|−|C|. If the sum reaches the threshold, the free final arm can be shortened at its end to attain exactly 193 vertices, provided enough of that arm remains; an earlier stop at a total of at least 193 already yields a 192-edge subpath of the verified assembled snake.
