# Completing the opposite face after three crossings

A coordinate used three times splits a snake into runs A, B, C, D, alternately in the two eight-cube faces. In the first face A and C are disjoint induced paths with no edge between them. In the second face B and D have the same property. The two supports share precisely three eight-bit addresses. An additional common address would give an extra crossing edge.

The 131 source paths have minimum coordinate-use counts one, two, three, or four in groups of 25, 88, 12, and six. Across the bank there are 22 occurrences of a three-crossed coordinate, twelve from sources with minimum three and ten from sources with minimum two. Fixing either face gives 44 indexed choices. Translation, coordinate permutation, reversing either run and exchanging the two runs reduce these to 21 distinct fixed forests. [All forests and sources](three-cross-faces-menu.json).

For each forest the experiment selects a completely new opposite face. Only the four tips of the fixed paths have a free incident edge, so exactly three of the four tip addresses must be selected across the face. The choice of the three is free. The fixed tip whose copy is absent is one global endpoint. In the opposite face choose exactly one global endpoint. Every selected vertex has degree two except the two endpoints, which have degree one. A disconnected solution consists of the endpoint-to-endpoint path and one or more cycles. Exclude the mutable vertices of each cycle from being selected together, and repeat. When a path is found, every pair of its vertices is tested for the induced-path condition. [Implementation](three_cross_faces.py).

Forest 8 comes from bank entries 111 and 112. In entry 111, coordinate 5 divides the path into 49, 49, 48 and 46 vertices. Fixing the first and third runs retains 97 vertices. On the opposite face the two runs together must increase from 95 to 96 vertices, giving 193 overall. A 192-vertex positive control recovers bank entry 112, up to isometry. At 193 vertices the longer unrestricted decision excluded the fixed forest. A second calculation split by the four possible fixed endpoints and independently returned UNSAT in all four branches. The outside endpoint has the same full nine-cube parity as the fixed endpoint because the total number of vertices is odd. [Unrestricted decision](three-cross-face-8-193-5000000.json), [positive control](three-cross-face-8-192-400000.json).

We then changed the fixed face itself. From each original forest remove one tip from each run, choosing either tip independently. Extend the resulting two runs by one step each, or by one and two steps in either order. Keep all resulting pairs of induced paths with no edge between them, and identify equivalent forests. Across all 21 inputs this yields 41 distinct forests outside the original menu. Three come from forest 8, each consisting of two 49-vertex runs. Those candidates need 95 freely chosen vertices in the opposite face. All four choices of the fixed endpoint remain available. [All 41 candidates and source indices](three-regrow--1-menu.json), [three candidates from entries 111 and 112](three-regrow-8-menu.json), [regrowth and decision procedure](regrow_three_faces.py).

The seven-face component bound independently requires each eight-dimensional side of any three-crossing 192-edge candidate to occupy between 81 and 112 vertices. [Geometric proof](DENSE_FACE_COMPONENTS.md).

## What survives the unrestricted face decisions?

All 21 fixed forests from the bank are excluded at 192 edges. The four possible fixed-face endpoints give disjoint cases. The saved census combines them with whole-forest results and lists the decisions for the 41 regrown forests separately. The full unrestricted formula and trimmed proof for forest 8 have been independently checked. [Current coverage](three-face-census.json), [coverage verifier](three_face_census.py), [forest 8 proof receipt](three-cross-face-8-193-5000000-checked.json).

A second experiment fixes the **two central runs in opposite faces** and permits both outer arms to change. The 22 source occurrences reduce to seven pairs under a common cube isometry and reversal. Each outer arm must start at the prescribed crossing, avoid the closed neighbourhood of the central run in its own face, and avoid all addresses of the opposite central run other than its root. The two outer arms must have disjoint addresses. These conditions leave the shapes and lengths of both arms free. All seven controls reproduce 191-edge bank snakes and pass a check of every vertex pair. All seven pairs fail at 192 edges. [The seven pairs](three-central-menu.json), [unrestricted two-arm decision](three_central_runs.py).

All 41 regrown fixed forests now also fail at 192 edges. For the last fixed endpoint of case 35, partition the opposite endpoint by its three lowest coordinate bits. The eight cases cover every address, and each was refuted. [Complete 21+41 census](three-face-census.json).

The seven central pairs supply twelve distinct rooted path shapes. We freely recombined those shapes, allowing repeats, and tried all 8! relative axis permutations at the common crossing for each of the 78 unordered pairs. Among 3,144,960 indexed placements, only 34 obey the required intersection and root-separation conditions. They give the seven original pairs and 27 alternatives. In eight alternatives the union of the two reachable outer domains has too few addresses. The other 19 have separately checked refutations at 191 edges with free outer arms. Trimming a free outer tip shows that these alternatives also fail at 192 edges. [Placement census](recombined-central-menu.json), [construction and decision driver](recombine_central.py).

Allowing either orientation of each central run and optionally deleting its last vertex gives 44 rooted shapes. All 39,916,800 relative placements yield 618 compatible pairs, including the seven original pairs. Among the 611 alternatives, 380 have too few reachable outer addresses for 193 vertices. The other 231 all returned UNSAT with both outer arms free. These 231 are solver decisions at the 192-edge target. The saved index identifies each pair, its domain sizes and its conflict count.
