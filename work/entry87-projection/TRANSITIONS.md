# Repair distance in the transition sequence

Coordinates changed across the whole path and transitions changed at individual positions are different metrics. We now compare the 192 symbols of the one-chord walk with the transition sequence of a genuine snake. All nine symbol names are available at every edited position.

**Interval translation lemma.** Replacing the transition at step $t$ from coordinate $a$ to $b$ translates every vertex at positions $t,\ldots,192$ by $e_a\mathbin{\oplus}e_b$. With multiple edits, each unedited segment is therefore a translate of the corresponding original segment. Its translation is the xor of the edit differences before that segment. Conversely, every ordered list of replacement positions and replacement symbols gives exactly the walk assembled from those translations. This follows by taking xor prefixes of the transition word.

For two translated segments with offsets $d_1,d_2$, a nonconsecutive pair $i,j$ violates inducedness exactly when the Hamming weight of $q_i\mathbin{\oplus}q_j\mathbin{\oplus}d_1\mathbin{\oplus}d_2$ is at most one. The relative offset has even parity. Precompute, for each even offset, a two-dimensional prefix sum counting all such nonconsecutive pairs over rectangles of the index grid. A segment passes precisely when its own rectangle and every rectangle against a previously closed segment have zero count. Consecutive edges are valid by construction.

For a fixed left boundary and offset, validity is monotone in the right boundary: adding indices only adds pairs. Binary search finds the longest possible unchanged segment; branch on every next edit position up to its first failing index and every replacement symbol distinct from the original. Thus pruning preserves all possible repairs in the specified transition Hamming sphere. Every long-range interaction with the previously selected prefix is retained.

Complete native runs exclude **all repairs using at most six transition substitutions**. At exactly six edits, the traversal tested 10,131,797,976 terminal branches after prefix pruning. There is no restriction on which coordinates these edits involve, the new transition types, the new endpoint, or the length of unchanged portions. The starting vertex can be normalized by translation.

The [search implementation](transition_ball.cpp) accepts a substitution count and a range for the first edited step; indices start at one. At radius zero the range is ignored. The [positive checks](positive_walk.py) corrupt and recover known 191-edge snakes, and separately verify the radius-one case by reconstructing every sequence. These native finite results are independent of the fixed-triple SAT results. A valid snake in the unrestricted cube can be farther away in both metrics.
