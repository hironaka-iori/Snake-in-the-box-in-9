# Four seven-dimensional paths with three vertical joins

Write the nine-cube as a seven-cube times a square. Visit the four layers in order 00, 01, 11, 10. Put one path in each layer, denoted A, B, C, D, including both endpoints. Their total number of vertices is the sum of their four sizes. Thus sizes 49, 48, 48, 48 would give a 192-edge snake. No member of the 131-source bank visits four distinct layers exactly once in any choice of two layer directions, as a direct count of coordinate runs shows.

**Joining criterion.** Orient each of the four paths. Consecutive paths must meet in exactly their joining endpoint, and A and D must be disjoint. A and C may overlap arbitrarily, and so may B and D. This criterion is necessary and sufficient. The only edges between neighbouring layers connect equal seven-bit addresses, and opposite layers have distance at least two. Within a layer each path is induced by assumption. The same layer mechanism appears in [Taylor's higher-dimensional constructions](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27483v1#S4). Here all four routes may have different shapes, orientations and lengths.

**All size profiles.** The seven-cube maximum is 50 edges, hence 51 vertices per layer. If the four deficits below 51 are d₀,d₁,d₂,d₃, they are nonnegative integers summing to 204−193 = 11. There are 364 ordered possibilities. None is invariant under reversal, since a reversal-invariant quadruple has even total deficit. Reversal therefore leaves exactly 182 cases. Every layer has between 40 and 51 vertices. [Seven-cube value and original references](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05119).

To obtain finite path menus, extract long contiguous pieces lying in seven-dimensional faces of the public source bank. A contiguous piece lies in such a face exactly when its transition word uses at most seven directions. A sliding window finds every inclusion-maximal piece: when a new eighth direction arrives, record the current piece and move its start past the earliest last occurrence among the seven used directions. Every seven-direction piece is contained in some recorded one. Keep all subpaths of the desired sizes, in both orientations, and normalize coordinates by order of first use. The inputs include 1,173 paths in dimension nine, 12 in dimension ten, 72 in dimension eleven, 34,688 in dimension twelve and 30 in dimension thirteen. Every extracted higher-dimensional word is rebuilt in a seven-cube and checked against every pair. [Source bank](https://github.com/Math-AI-Caltech/Snake-in-the-Box), [extractor](extract_seven.cpp), [data generation](highdim_seven.py), [293 extracted paths and source indices](highdim-seven-39.json).

The complete finite-menu construction tries every relative cube isometry by naming each new arm direction the first time it occurs. In a second, third or fourth layer, its hinge fixes the translation. Cut off a partial word when it uses a forbidden address in a neighbouring layer. The fourth layer also avoids all of the first. Across the 182 size profiles the search visits 46,181,195 partial states and 1,032 full intermediate placements. Zero complete four-layer snakes reach 193 vertices in these menus. The exact search concerns four paths all belonging to the extracted menus. Arbitrary seven-cube paths give a larger problem. [Search](four_layer_bank.cpp), [independent assembly and all-pair checks](four_layer_bank.py), [size-profile enumeration](four_layer_lengths.py), [profiles 0 through 90](four-layer-lengths-highdim-seven-39-0-91-180000000.json), [profiles 91 through 181](four-layer-lengths-highdim-seven-39-91-182-180000000.json).

**Free middle and last layers.** Fix A and C, two opposite layers, and orient them so their connecting ends are a, c and the other end of C is d. Let U be all seven-bit addresses outside the union of A and C. For a valid join a cannot be in C, and c,d cannot be in A. Then B may be any induced path from a to c inside U ∪ {a,c}, and D may independently be any induced path starting at d inside U ∪ {d}. The independent maxima add exactly to |A|+|C| in the vertex count. This follows because B and D are opposite layers and share no edge even when they occupy identical addresses. This splits a 512-vertex construction into two longest-path problems on much smaller graphs, while allowing newly constructed shapes on both sides. [Anchor placement](zipper.cpp), [optimization, independent joining and all-pair verification](zipper.py).

As a constructive check, fixing only the first three shapes and freely rebuilding the last layer produces a verified **190-edge** snake with layer sizes 49, 48, 47, 47. Comparison by the full transition word identifies it as a previously known 190-edge symmetry class. The fourth layer may have any shape and its endpoint is free. [Reconstruction](free_last_layer.py), [190-edge checked witness](four-layer-190-control.json). Simultaneously moving all four layers around this explicit source retains a starting point two edges below the research target.
