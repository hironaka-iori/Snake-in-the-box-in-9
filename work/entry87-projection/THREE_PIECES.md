# Two independent hinges in the 180 one-chord walks

Split a 193-vertex one-chord walk into a left arm ending at position l, a spine from l through r, and a right arm starting at r, with 0<l<r<192. Apply any cube isometry to each of the three pieces, keeping the copies of either hinge joined. A common inverse isometry fixes the spine. Thus the two remaining pieces have independently chosen coordinate permutations, and their translations are determined by their hinges. This operation can change every vertex of both arms simultaneously.

The source chord rules out a division immediately if both of its ends lie within any one of the three pieces. Otherwise each piece is induced internally. Grow an arm by naming coordinates in order of first appearance; assign each new name an unused target direction. Discard any arm vertex meeting the closed neighbourhood of a non-hinge spine vertex. The relationship with its own hinge is inherited from the isometric source arm. For each surviving pair of arms, their non-hinge vertex sets must avoid each other's closed neighbourhoods. This condition, together with the two separate spine tests, checks every possible cross-piece chord or identification. A valid pair constructs the snake. The program also reconstructs a valid control by undoing a deliberately introduced rotation of a source arm and independently checks every pair of its vertices.

Across all 180 one-chord inputs, no such construction with at least 35 spine vertices yields a valid 192-edge snake. For spine lengths 35 through 89 there are 1,287,000 indexed hinge pairs and 112,747,398 final arm tests. The full sweep for lengths 90 through 191 takes another 1,406,071 final arm tests. [Enumerator](double_pivots.cpp), [input and isometry verifier](double_pivots.py), [shorter spines, first half](double-pivots-35-89-0-90.json), [shorter spines, second half](double-pivots-35-89-90-180.json), [longer spines, complete bank](double-pivots-90-191-0-180.json).

The optional indexed pair search separates the right-arm vertex sets by whether they contain a chosen address. At every node of this binary tree store the intersection of all sets below it. If that intersection meets the left arm's forbidden neighbourhood, the entire node can be skipped. When all the sets remaining at a leaf are equal, it suffices to test one of them. This is the same exact geometric compatibility predicate with fewer full mask comparisons.

### Patch size in Hamming distance

A second repair allows each eligible vertex to move to any of the 36 addresses at Hamming distance two, or to remain at its old address. Require all consecutive pairs to be adjacent and all nonconsecutive pairs to be distinct and nonadjacent. Across the 180 one-chord walks, permitting movement at every position within eight chronological places of either end of the chord fails in all cases. Iterating fixed-vertex constraints directly empties a domain for 162 inputs, and the other 18 formulas return UNSAT. For row 118, the entry-87 near walk, a radius of 28 chronological places around either chord end is also excluded. These statements allow the starting vertex to move whenever its position is in the eligible region.
