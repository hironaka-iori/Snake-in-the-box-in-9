# Moving the middle while retaining the two crossings

Fix the two crossing addresses of a stored two-crossing 191-edge path. Every replacement middle between them has the same length parity. Gaining exactly one edge therefore requires an odd change in the number of vertices on the outer arms.

We removed an odd number t of vertices from the free ends of the two outer arms, splitting the removals in every way that kept both roots. We then allowed an arbitrary induced middle between the same roots, with t+1 additional vertices. The new middle must contain at most 99 vertices. This permits t=1 for the 84 sources with a 96-vertex middle, t=1 or 3 for the two sources with a 94-vertex middle, and t=1, 3 or 5 for the two sources with a 93-vertex middle. A successful reconstruction would have 193 vertices.

For each choice the available middle addresses are exactly the complement of the retained outer sites together with the two crossing addresses. A connecting induced path of the required size in this domain is necessary and sufficient. Its middle lies in one face, and its only common addresses with the two outer paths are the prescribed crossings. Every complete path is tested against all vertex pairs. Normalization permits translation, coordinate permutation and exchange of the two ends of the full path. [Generator and reconstruction](replace_middle.py), [explicit domains from 200 indexed edits](replaced-middle-menu.json).

The 200 edits give 191 distinct normalized cases. Their required middle sizes are 95 in four cases, 96 in four, 97 in eight, 98 in 167, and 99 in eight. **All 191 fail.** For each formula the original and trimmed proofs were independently checked. The trimmed proofs use 66,806,818 resolution steps in total, with no dynamic cycle exclusions. [Proof producer](replacement_proofs.py), [cases 0–47](replacement-proofs-0-48.json), [48–95](replacement-proofs-48-96.json), [96–143](replacement-proofs-96-144.json), [144–190](replacement-proofs-144-191.json).

There is a further consequence for the higher target. Keeping both full outer arms and increasing the old middle by two vertices would give 193 edges. Trimming one outer endpoint of that construction would yield one of the excluded 192-edge cases. Thus the same certificates also rule out that two-edge gain, with both original crossings retained, for every one of the 88 sources.
