# Joining two subpaths from unrelated sources

Allow the first 96 edges of a putative 192-edge snake to be any contiguous 96-edge subpath of a saved snake. Allow its last 96 edges to come from any other saved snake, with any translation, reflection, reversal and coordinate permutation. The two halves share the middle vertex and have no other prescribed relative position.

Across the 131 saved 191-edge paths and 1,042 published 190-edge paths there are 36,780 distinct rooted, directed 96-edge transition words up to coordinate renaming. The search exhausted every pairing and relative isometry. No such pair joins at a common middle vertex to make a 192-edge snake.

Translate the common hinge to zero, and place the left half in its normalized orientation. Subsequences are taken at every position and read in both directions. The non-hinge vertices of the left half are pairwise nonconsecutive with every non-hinge vertex on the right. Their closed cube neighbourhoods must therefore be avoided by the entire right half after its hinge. Conversely, avoiding those closed neighbourhoods is sufficient because both arms are induced and their pre-existing adjacencies to the hinge are correct. This gives a direct test with no relaxation.

Insert all the normalized right words into a prefix tree. When a new coordinate first occurs on the right, try every unused physical direction. An old coordinate has exactly one image. This accounts for all cube isometries, and an initial segment intersecting a forbidden neighbourhood rules out all its descendants. For the 1,173-source bank the tree has 1,488,542 nodes. The complete search visits 4,543,630,022 pairs of partial placement and tree node. Two halves cut from a known 191-edge snake serve as a constructive control. Every constructed path is checked against all vertex pairs.

[Enumerator](mixed_halves.cpp), [input generation, normalization and checks](mixed_halves.py), [complete 1,173-source run](mixed-halves-96-96-1-1173.json), [131-source run](mixed-halves-96-96-1-131.json). On the 131 longest sources the same conclusion also holds for [90 and 102 edges](mixed-halves-90-102-1-131.json) and [80 and 112 edges](mixed-halves-80-112-1-131.json).

An independent reconstruction from the shorter sources preserves the ordered eight-dimensional trace and reassigns the erased coordinate using the [crossing-parity equations](PUNCTURED_LIFT.md). All 9,378 traces are included. For 9,106 of them the maximum is 190 edges, and for 272 it is 191 edges. The latter arise from 256 distinct input rows. One representative per improving trace was reconstructed and checked against every vertex pair. All 272 representatives match members of the 191-edge bank after coordinate renaming, translation and optional reversal. [Computation](projection_seeds190.py), [complete census](projected-190-census.json), [public bank](https://github.com/Math-AI-Caltech/Snake-in-the-Box).

The test also permits a newly grown bridge between two independent arms. Starting from the chosen left hinge, recursively append any neighbouring vertex outside the closed neighbourhoods of earlier vertices other than the current hinge. Before each extension, add the closed neighbourhood of the old hinge to the forbidden set. At the end of the bridge, enumerate all right arms with the same prefix tree. Known paths are reconstructed after removing a bridge from their centres. With arbitrary contiguous arms of the 131-source bank, the [96 + 1 + 95](mixed-halves-96-95-1-131-bridge1.json) and [95 + 2 + 95](mixed-halves-95-95-1-131-bridge2.json) families are exhausted. Restricting each arm to start at a bank endpoint also exhausts [92 + 8 + 92](mixed-halves-92-92-0-131-bridge8.json) on the 131 sources and [95 + 3 + 94](mixed-halves-95-94-0-1173-bridge3.json) on the 1,173 sources.
