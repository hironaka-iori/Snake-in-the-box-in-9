# V2 independent review: endpoint star and disjoint degree-three groups

Reviewed `graph_solver.py` SHA256: `86d42cd0bbb32c6c34995fb61e48815a4818290b76469f500c6f47511c06f180`. The solver was read only. Independent tests and receipts: `review_v2.py`, `review_v2.json`.

For a rooted induced continuation with head h, h is selected and is an endpoint. In A={h} union N_R(h), at most two vertices can be selected, so at least max(deg_R(h)-1,0) are omitted. The implementation removes this entire group before packing other patterns. In the remainder, every four-vertex group consisting of a center and three of its neighbors must omit at least one vertex: otherwise the selected induced graph gives that center degree at least three. Additional edges between its neighbors cannot invalidate this argument. Each group is removed before another is packed, so omissions are counted in disjoint vertex sets. The resulting |R|-endpoint_loss-number_of_packed_groups is therefore safe. Greedy order affects strength, not soundness.

`claw` mode takes separate necessary reach, parity, leaf and claw bounds; `hybrid` additionally takes the separate block bound. The source does not add a cycle penalty to a claw penalty. This is correct because their omitted vertices might overlap. The endpoint-star deduction is valid only for a fixed continuation beginning at h, not for an arbitrary path with h in its interior.

Independent direct recursion checked 23,120 arbitrary-graph/root cases (all five-vertex graphs plus 3,000 random six-vertex graphs), every nonempty Q4 domain with its canonical root (65,535 cases), all returned group disjointness and adjacency conditions, 24,976 packed degree-three groups, and 80,775 endpoint stars. A further 12,000 complete searches across claw/hybrid, memo on/off, and targets at/above the exact oracle optimum all agree; returned paths pass independent pairwise checks. These searches exercised 570 claw prunes and zero memo hits, so they do not add dynamic memo-hit coverage beyond the earlier mathematical review. All PASS.

This rule is a conditional prefix pruning bound, not a new global Q9 bound. Even optimizing an unrestricted disjoint four-vertex packing allows only floor(512/4)=128 such groups in Q9. Reserving the full endpoint star leaves 502 vertices, hence at most 125 groups and endpoint loss 8, giving a root capacity at least 379. Thus this root-only disjoint-group bound alone cannot exclude a 235-edge snake. No new search was launched for this review.
