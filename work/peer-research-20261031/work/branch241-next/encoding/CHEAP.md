# Cheap support-line objective (r6)

Implemented `cheap_objective.py` and executed bank67 positive controls for1,3,7,9 internal centres. All gate witnesses satisfy the complete emitted gate CNF, and every centre score equals `Charges.cheap()['r6']['values']` exactly. Adaptive dominance is also checked with integer cross multiplication. Nine-centre total2412 at scale21; individual scores281,233,200,321,211,244,421,267,234. This is a feasible selection/control, not a universal lower bound.

The nine-centre geometry needs477 vertex variables,3950 shared exact-empty gates,4428 variables total and28524 clauses. Without cross-centre sharing there would be9*792=7128 objective gates. One centre uses36 singleton gates (three absent corners, centre fixed selected),252 distance-one empty-square gates and504 distance-one empty3-cube gates. The AND definitions are biconditionals, not one-way objective flags. The export includes raw clauses, base-variable mapping and merged positive weighted `(literal,coefficient)` terms. It deliberately omits the full geometric Encoding and the PB cap; callers must allocate using the existing fresh/add/var namespace, and add the existing induced-path clauses before a charge-bound search.

All face/cube gates are cached by the sorted complete vertex set, and weighted occurrences are merged by literal. A full radius4 ball around each centre is constructed; missing any required corner raises an exception. No cube is clipped at the window boundary. A radius3 local model is insufficient for distance-one3-cubes.

`cheap_objective_bank67.json` is the emitted nine-centre gate-CNF/objective example; `cheap_objective_controls.json` records exact controls. NoSAT dependency, noBDD/full-window solve, noUNSAT certificate orLean build was run. To certify a lower boundT, attach a weighted cap `sum terms <= T-1`, reconstruct static CNF including geometric clauses/lazy cuts, then establish gate/PB semantics in Lean before replaying LRAT. The generic new mathematical support line is not yet formalized by this encoding prototype.
