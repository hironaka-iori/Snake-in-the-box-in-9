- [Nine-dimensional snake witness](snake9_witness.json) records 191 edges, 192 vertices and the check of all 18,336 unordered vertex pairs.
- [Eight-dimensional witness](snake8_witness.json) and [nine-dimensional bank audit](snake9_bank_audit.json) provide paths and comparison data.
- [Milestone guide](../milestones/README.md) links to more recent calculations and their individual proof records.
- [Manuscript guide](../paper/README.md) contains the current written results and their PDFs.
