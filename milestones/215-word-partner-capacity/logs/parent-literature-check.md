# Parent-class literature check

The search for a different 98-edge parent should not be confused with the already established optimum in dimension eight. The publisher's accessible abstract for Östergård and Pettersson confirms the exhaustive upper bound 98 and describes classification by recursive construction and canonical augmentation:

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00373-014-1423-3

The complete article was subscription-only in the retrieved page. The author's publication list and Aalto research portal supplied bibliographic metadata, but no accessible class catalogue was found in this bounded check. The dissertation landing page returned 403 through the web tool. Consequently this check establishes neither uniqueness of the 98-edge parent nor a census of its equivalence classes.

Orland et al. explicitly note that reusing lower-dimensional optimal snakes can miss optima; their discussion of the known 98-edge parent does not provide a complete classification of its peers:

https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15270v3

The existing manuscript correctly cites the dimension-eight upper bound as a literature result. No manuscript correction or new numerical bound follows from this check. The construction search remains restricted to changed coordinate histories of one saved parent, and its outcomes must keep that scope.
