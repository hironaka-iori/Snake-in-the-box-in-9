# Round 244. Reconstructing a 192-edge path from published 190-edge paths

The construction source is the authors' separate collection of 1,042 nine-dimensional 190-edge paths. The [computation](parents.py) reads the member named snakes_dim9_190.csv in the published archive; its exact hash is in the [result](results.json). Each order is checked for range, distinct vertices, consecutive adjacency and absence of nonconsecutive adjacency. Transition words normalized up to coordinate renaming and reversal give 1,042 distinct classes. [Orland et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15270) · [Source archive at the inspected revision](https://github.com/Math-AI-Caltech/Snake-in-the-Box/blob/46c765399353a588ea50444a5d6610f53102b1c2/record_snakes.csv.zip)

## Extensions and free opposite-side reconstruction

First we tried every legal endpoint addition at either end and every next addition from each resulting class. The first additions recovered all 131 published 191-edge classes. None yielded another class or a 192-edge path. Coordinate renaming, translation and reversal preserve endpoint extendibility, so only one representative of each intermediate class is required.

A 190-edge path has 96 vertices of its endpoints' parity and 95 of the opposite parity. A 192-edge target with its endpoints on the *other* parity may use the old 96-set as its minority. We held this set fixed and allowed the entire new 97-vertex majority class, both endpoints and the path order to change. There are 588 distinct labelled 96-sets. The exact fixed-minority contraction requires a Hamiltonian path whose ends can each be attached to a singleton-neighbour majority label. In 302 cases its graph leaves force incompatible ends. Seven have too few eligible endpoints. In 211, propagation of mandatory degree-two edges in the dummy-augmented graph gives a contradiction. The remaining 68 returned native solver contradictions. All 588 cases were excluded. The calculation uses the same graph criterion as the coil experiment with different input sets.

The count reconstruction theorem also quantifies the required change in this orientation. No odd-side or even-side eight-count trade is compatible with these 588 sets: across 71,124,480 signed placement comparisons the minimum conflict count is five. The [signed-orbit result](balanced-orbit.json) and its [calculation](support_bound.py) use the complete four-block orbit of Round 241. Keeping the 96-set fixed is excluded above, and a nonzero balanced change cannot have exactly nine counts by Round 242. Therefore at least ten opposite-side counts change on account of the balanced class. The class growing from 95 to 97 has indicator-sum difference two, so the sum of its neighbour-count differences is eighteen. The weight-two moment matrix over the field of five elements has nonzero scalar diagonal eighteen and vanishing off-diagonal. Its rank nine requires at least nine nonzero positions. In fact nine are also impossible: the square-matrix argument puts their support at \(N(q)\), and the antipodal inverse gives
\[
0=144w(p)-16\!\sum w=144w(p)-288
\]
at every \(p\in N(q)\), since ternarity and mod-two locality give zero at its antipode. Consequently \(w=2\mathbf1_{N(q)}\) and invertibility would give \(f=2\mathbf1_{\{q\}}\), contradicting ternarity. This orientation thus requires at least **twenty** changed labelled counts relative to each parent. The statement concerns this orientation of a 192-edge target.

## Retaining both original endpoints

In the other orientation we retained both original endpoints and made the original 96-set a subset of the target's 97-vertex majority class. Consider every possible single addition on that parity; the 96 minority vertices and the entire order are free. For a fixed new majority class \(V\), a minority vertex can only be an internal label with exactly two neighbours in \(V\). Build a graph on \(V\) joining those two neighbours for every such label. A Hamiltonian path between the specified endpoints is necessary and sufficient. Sufficiency follows by choosing one label for every used pair and interleaving; each label meets exactly its two specified majority vertices. All same-parity adjacencies are absent. The [fixed-end search](fixed_ends.py) checks forced degrees, then uses an edge-selection model for unresolved graphs with degree one at the ends and two elsewhere. A disconnected model is cut by requiring an edge to leave its endpoint-containing component. Every hypothetical target survives each such cut.

There are 774 distinct pairs consisting of the labelled parent 96-set and its unordered endpoint pair. Each admits 160 additions. All **123,840** contracted graphs have a vertex of degree less than its required degree (one for each endpoint, two for each internal vertex). The solver was unnecessary. Thus retaining both old endpoints and the whole old majority class prevents reaching 192, regardless of all minority choices. [Exact outcome](fixed-ends.json)
