# Independent source review

A bounded read-only review checked the generator against the existing Lean
table literals. The review started no compiler and changed no source file.
Compilation receipts provide the separate verification record.

The review compared all 791 contexts with the ordered 55-rule catalogue.
Selected lists, block/class pairs, class roots, endpoint bases and unique
longest-suffix history updates agreed. The potential inequality uses the
target potential on the left and the source potential on the right. Completed
and conditional floors are paired with slopes 483 and 496 and their respective
potential modules. The original generated import graph had no duplicate
declarations or missing direction-theorem imports. Its equations covered all
505 directions and all 791 contexts.

The first finite candidate, direction 73/history 58, did not test a history
change in the final weight: its history credit was 4 or 5, below floors 12
and 16. No finite compilation of that candidate was started. The selector was
revised to require a strict weight increase on an accepted code after all
other credits are included, together with an accepted code where the caps
fail. It selected direction 99/history 37. Exact numerical enumeration agrees
with the previously audited accepted-code hash and finds four accepted codes
where the capped credit increases both profile weights from a credit of 18
to 19. The context has 6,043 accepted codes.

The final context-specific reduction was added after this read-only review.
Its separate Lean receipt, rather than this review, checks the claim that
both profile weights coincide on direction 99/history 37. The proof summary
audits declaration uniqueness and coverage again after all additions.

The generated sources introduce no mathematical definition. They reuse the
single generic evaluator in ContextWeights101. The paper and its notation
are unchanged in this milestone.
