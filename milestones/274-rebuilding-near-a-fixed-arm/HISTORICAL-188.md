# The two 188-edge constructions

**Casella’s 97-edge arm has exact maximum 188 at its published crossing, with the opposite arm unrestricted.** The other Round 273 construction also attains its exact maximum of 188 at its specified crossing. Each lower bound uses a verified 90-edge partner. Orient the fixed arm from the crossing towards its free endpoint. Deleting its last edge leaves the entire opposite arm and the crossing intact. The joined snake loses exactly one edge. Thus an upper bound $u$ for the 96-edge prefix gives an upper bound $u+1$ for the full arm. This argument applies to every induced opposite arm in the eight-dimensional face. Its reconstructed Boolean formula and contradiction certificate passed independent replay, giving the exact ceiling of 188.
