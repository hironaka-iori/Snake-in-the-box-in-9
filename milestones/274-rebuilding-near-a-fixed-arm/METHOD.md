# Which arm changes are included?

A snake is an induced path. Its length counts edges. Write each of the two faces of a chosen ninth coordinate as a copy of $Q_8$. Translate the crossing to zero. If $A$ and $B$ are ordered outwards from the crossing, the joined path visits $A$ in reverse, crosses once, and then follows $B$. It is induced precisely when both face paths are induced and their projected vertex sets intersect only at zero. The length is the sum of the two arm lengths plus one. Since an arm in $Q_8$ has at most 98 edges, both arms of a 192-edge join must have at least 93 edges. The three possible unordered splits are $93+1+98$, $94+1+97$, and $95+1+96$.

Fix an ordered eight-dimensional seed $S$ in a literal cube labelling. For a candidate arm $P$, the number of omissions is $|V(S)\setminus V(P)|$. If the seed has $s$ edges, a candidate with $p$ edges and $r$ omissions adds exactly $p-s+r$ vertices. For a 94-edge seed, omitting one site and finishing with 93 edges leaves room for zero additions. Only the two end truncations stay connected. A candidate may change its crossing, its endpoints and the order of the retained pieces. An isometric copy of the seed gives the same problem by applying the inverse isometry to both faces.

## Completeness of the enumeration

1. Choose exactly which seed positions are omitted. Every other seed vertex is mandatory. Because the seed was induced, the graph on the mandatory vertices is a disjoint union of paths, including single vertices. Every edge of every component must also be an edge of the final path. A component therefore occurs as a consecutive interval of the final path.
2. Enumerate every order of the components and both orientations of each nonsingleton. Reversal allows one of the two opposite component orders to be discarded. Between successive components, build a bridge one vertex at a time. A new vertex may touch only its predecessor among the vertices already reserved. Its final vertex must also touch the entry of the next component. Reserve all mandatory vertices from the start so that a contact with a future component is detected immediately.
3. After the components have been connected, grow the two ends in turn to the length limit. Exclude every original omitted vertex from the additions. In an induced path each of these growth operations is legal when performed along the path. Conversely the neighbour conditions check every edge to a vertex already present. This proves completeness and inducedness in each omission class. Save paths in the orientation beginning at the numerically smaller endpoint.
4. An independent enumeration starts with the same mandatory set and adds adjacent vertices in any order. It rejects an addition that would produce degree three or close a cycle. Every completed path has a construction order from the mandatory set because its graph is connected. The enumeration merges identical occupied sets. A connected set with maximum degree two and two endpoints supplies a unique path up to reversal. For each omitted set the replay compares the count, and for each omission level it compares the ordered-path digest, the counts by length and the shape multiplicities. The output paths undergo an independent check of every pair of vertices.

For the opposite-face search, both endpoints of each reconstructed arm are possible crossings. Translate the endpoint to zero and number axes in order of first use. Two such ordered paths give the same rooted problem exactly when these normalized vertex lists coincide. Candidate partner placements and direct search both output ordered nine-dimensional paths. A contradiction at target 192 is also an upper bound for longer joins at that crossing, since the opposite arm of any longer join could be shortened to produce exactly 192.

A simpler upper bound sometimes follows by shortening the fixed arm. If a rooted parent extends a certified rooted prefix by $d$ edges, deleting those free-end edges from any full join gives a join at the prefix that is exactly $d$ edges shorter. A prefix ceiling $U$ therefore gives a ceiling of $U+d$ at the full parent. The saved coordinate maps verify that the prefix starts from the same crossing. This argument settles the published Casella crossing. [Two 188-edge cases](HISTORICAL-188.md)
