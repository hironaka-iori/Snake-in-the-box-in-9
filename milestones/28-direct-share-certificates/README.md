# Direct tests and certificates for empty-square shares

Checkpoint: 21 October 2026. This milestone follows the empty-square route in milestone 27. It adds one Lean-checked geometric exclusion, three Lean-checked arithmetic lemmas, and a local counterexample to a proposed uniform share bound. No new global bound or longer snake is claimed.

## Main results

1. **Checked window exclusion.** Six successive transition directions `a,b,c,a,d,b`, for four distinct coordinates, cannot have singleton-square counts `(2,4,5,3,3)` at the five internal vertices of a snake. `lean/WindowExclusion.lean` proves the finite refutation and transfers it to arbitrary snakes through the coordinate-permutation and translation bridge. Both axiom audits contain only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
2. **The exclusion needs no share premise.** The initial share CNF has 43,187 variables and 535,049 clauses. Its checked DRAT core contains only 1,795 original window clauses, with 1,134 active variables. These were extracted, renumbered, and proved inconsistent through a fresh LRAT certificate replayed in Lean. The final theorem does not depend on the experimental share encoder.
3. **A uniform share lower bound of 108 fails in the tested local model.** For transition pattern `0,1,2,3,0,4` and counts `(4,5,3,3,5)`, the saved 119-vertex configuration has shares `(22,29,9,9,35)`, totaling **104**. It lies in the 421-vertex union of radius-four balls, is acyclic with selected degree at most two, and has only two degree-one selected vertices in the interior, numbered 27 and 134. Boundary endpoints remain unconstrained. This is a local forest, with no claim that it extends to a complete snake. The witness and all counts are independently checked by `scripts/verify.py`; this finite witness check has not been ported to Lean.
4. **Independent evidence for an existing numerical premise.** The exact-quotient CP-SAT model excludes share at most 300 for pattern `0,1,2,3,0,4`, counts `(2,[6,21],2,2,[6,21])`. This supports the previously recorded lower bound 301, but its solver result has no Lean proof here. The 163, 245, and adjacent-pair 10 targets remain UNKNOWN in these runs.

## Why the search direction changed

The current five-window numerical table admits an eleven-position periodic state sequence of mean singleton count 42/11, with every recorded five-window share lower bound equal to zero. The saved cycle satisfies the 739 listed exclusions. This is an obstruction to that specific table, excluding separate pair/single-vertex share premises; it is neither a snake nor a limitation of every radius-four argument. Its feasibility was checked exactly after the graph search. The search explored a pruned graph with 44,064 states and 264,275 retained edges; no minimum-mean optimality claim is used.

Of the eleven missing windows, seven were excluded by the share CP-SAT probe. Screening all eleven without shares then excluded nine. Eight of these nine remain solver-only results; the first is the new Lean theorem above. The two remaining windows have saved acyclic local witnesses. A share-capped search on the first of these gives the total 104.

The resulting priority is precise: first remove windows already impossible under ordinary path constraints, then spend share-certification effort on the surviving windows. A solver exclusion under a share cap must be checked for dependence on share clauses before interpreting it as evidence for the empty-square contribution.

## Arithmetic and validation

`lean/ShareArithmetic.lean` checks the quotient interval characterization, the threshold equivalence `t <= N/d` iff `t*d <= N` for positive d, and equivalence of a nonnegative sum cap K with the same cap after truncating each summand at K+1. These justify using a finite static threshold encoding without an unproved geometric cutoff. They do not certify every clause generated by the new Python encoder.

Completed validation:

- 56 fixed-selection controls in Q3, Q4, and four published Q9 examples, tested at the exact share and one below it;
- four semantic-assignment checks over all 535,049 clauses, allowing failure only of the deliberately mismatched count/share premises;
- independent geometry/count checks for three saved local witnesses, and checks that all 305 saved cycle cuts are connected induced cycles;
- an external DRAT check and Lean kernel replay for the extracted ordinary-window exclusion;
- five new theorem axiom audits: three arithmetic statements, the finite refutation, and its snake interpretation.

These controls are evidence about the implementations, not exhaustive proofs of the share encoder. The old initial CP model, which used only square cycle cuts, is retained in `scripts/direct_initial.py` and `data/initial/`; the later model also adds six-cycle cuts. Each run records its source hash.


The verifier uses only Python's standard library and reads compressed certificates directly when raw copies are absent. The two build scripts create a fresh support directory and then compile the certificate. Their dependency paths must already exist. Source generation uses the repository's `gen_lean_sat.py` and `gen_lean_lrat.py`, native CaDiCaL 2.1.2 for the final LRAT proof, python-sat 1.9.dev17/CaDiCaL 1.5.3 for experiments, and OR-Tools 9.15 for the independent model. Python must run in UTF-8 mode to preserve generated Lean text on Windows.

The full CNFs and proof traces are losslessly compressed; `data/archives.json` hashes both compressed and original bytes. The source of the external checker is `work/keep96/vendor/drat-trim/drat-trim.c` in the repository, SHA-256 `d834b649f437e091597f5347f259b9f681087f89ca0844d0cee250a1a1a0c2ee`. It was built locally with Zig 0.13.0 using `cc -O2 -Dgetc_unlocked=getc`. Its command is:

```text
drat-trim certificates/obstruction0.cnf certificates/obstruction0.drat -c certificates/obstruction0.core.cnf -L certificates/obstruction0.lrat
```

`scripts/plain_core.py` prepares the share-free Lean certificate from that core. It verifies clause provenance before generation. The static share encoder refuses to certify an incremental proof containing late cycle clauses; `scripts/certify_share.py` instead restarts proof generation from the complete clause set.
