# Positive remainder support

Every positive natural-number corner remainder is at least1. Therefore the number of positive remainders is at most their sum. If L<=3, at most3 corners have positive remainder, leaving at least509 of the512 cube vertices with zero remainder. A zero remainder gives d(q) dividing12e(q); this assertion permits d=0 only if e=0. For the nontrivial snake case, the established small-loss domination theorem already excludes d=0.

At degree5, e mod5=2 would force12e mod5=4, impossible when one corner's loss is bounded by total loss<=3. Thus the restriction is modular, without an assumed cap on e. The core list and modular statements have separate Lean checks. The Finset/actual-snake wrapper depends on preceding integration modules and must retain its own status.
