# Toward an unconditional 244: kernel-checked refutations of the (2,2,3) windows

`upper_244_of_no_two_two_three` is conditional on no snake containing three consecutive internal vertices with local counts 2, 2, 3. Two radius-three window CNFs carry that premise (path vertices `0,1,3,7,6` and `0,1,3,7,15`, centres 1, 3, 7). Their DRAT refutations were checked by drat-trim only.

**G2, the refutation (done).** The repository now proves both CNFs unsatisfiable in Lean, with every unit-propagation step checked by the kernel and only the standard axioms:
- `w223_rep_unsat : WinCNF.unsat W223Rep.F`: 1,629 steps, 148,089 hints, 1,245 used clauses;
- `w223_dis_unsat : WinCNF.unsat W223Dis.F`: 17,101 steps, 926,950 hints, 2,172 used clauses.

Where things are:
- Lean modules: `work/spectral/stronger/WinCNF.lean`, `WinRup.lean`, `WinRupCmd.lean`, `W223Rep*.lean`, `W223Dis*.lean`, `W223.lean`.
- Clause semantics: `WinCNF` (`Lit := Int`, `clauseSat`, `unsat F := ∀ τ, (∀ c ∈ F, clauseSat τ c) → False`).
- Bitmask RUP checker with its soundness proof: `WinRup.rup_sound`.
- `WinRupCmd.rup_step` only builds proof terms and hands them to `addDecl`, so the kernel evaluates every check. A corrupted step is rejected; no `ofReduceBool` or native code is used.
- Each `*Inputs.lean` holds the clauses the proof uses, their indices in the full CNF and their kinds, with SHA-256 hashes of both in its header.
- The reproduction driver checks the 21 modules in about 200 s (`outputs/strengthened-direction-232-reproduction-manifest.json`).
- Tools in this folder:
  - `gen2.py` rebuilds the CNF with `wincert.Encoding` and trims the proof to LRAT with drat-trim `-L`;
  - `export_lean.py` writes the Lean modules;
  - `check_data.py` independently checks that the Lean clauses match the CNF file.

**Normalisation (done).** `work/spectral/stronger/CubePermutation.lean` shows that cube automorphisms map snakes to snakes and keep `pathDir` (mapped by the coordinate permutation), `localAlpha` and the (2,2,3) window. Any window with pairwise distinct directions can be normalised.

**G1 and assembly (done, 20 October 2026).**
- `W223G1Defs/Data/Sound.lean`: a kernel-decidable clause checker `check`, with the soundness lemma `sound : Hyp T S → blocksOK T → check T c → clauseSat (tau T S) c`. The tables in `W223G1Data` (generated by `gendata.py`) are untrusted hints that the kernel re-checks.
- `W223G1CheckRep/Dis.lean`: every used clause passes the checker (`decide +kernel`).
- `W223G1Snake.lean`, `W223G1Inst.lean`: a snake through `0,1,3,7,(6|15)` with counts 2,2,3 satisfies the hypotheses. This includes `localAlpha` equal to the number of free corners off the snake.
- `UpperBound244.lean`:
  - the window-shape case split, the normalisation and the G2 refutations give `W223G1.no_223`;
  - then `Snake9.upper_bound_244 : ∀ {m} (p : Snake m), m ≤ 244` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_244`.
- Reproduction: `outputs/strengthened-244-reproduction-manifest.json`, 353 steps, success, standard axioms only. The G1 modules take about 60 s and G2 about 200 s.

**Result: 191 <= a(9) <= 244, unconditional and Lean-checked.**

## 242 and 241 unconditional (20 October 2026)

The same machinery discharges every premise of `upper_242_of_certified_windows` and `upper_241_of_certified_windows`:
- `WinG1Gen.lean`: a generic G1 layer whose counter blocks are classified by the kernel as endpoint budget, `alpha <= hi` or `alpha >= lo`, so ranges such as 4..6 work.
- `WinShape.lean`:
  - chord exclusion for direction patterns `abcab`;
  - normalisation forwards or backwards, which covers the 3-window shapes 0120/0123 and the 4-window shapes 01203/01230/01231/01234;
  - reversed patterns are read backwards.
- 12 window cases, each with its inputs, kernel-checked RUP steps (`WinRupImp.rup_imp_step`, curried hint arguments, about 40% smaller objects) and a G1 check: `W224*`, `W232*`, `W323*`, `W41c*`, `W2423A/B/C*`, `W3242A*`. The (2,2,3) refutations are reused for (3,2,2).
- The final theorems:
  - `UpperBound242.lean`: `Snake9.upper_bound_242`;
  - `UpperBound241.lean`: `Snake9.upper_bound_241` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_241`.

The generator `u242/gen.py` checks the SHA-256 hashes of the committed certificate CNF and DRUP files and that `wincert.Encoding` reproduces each CNF up to the cycle cuts. It then trims the proofs with `drat-trim -L` (`u242/trim.sh`) and writes the modules.

Reproduction `outputs/strengthened-241-reproduction-manifest.json`: 606 steps, success, 26 minutes with `--jobs 2`. The 255 new modules take 2,741 module-seconds; the largest single refutation is (3,2,3) with distinct directions, 575 s. Every audited theorem uses only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

**Result: 191 <= a(9) <= 241, unconditional and Lean-checked.**

What is not re-derived in Lean: the CNF encodings themselves. As for 244, the kernel checks that the clauses actually used by each refutation hold for every snake (G1) and that they are unsatisfiable (G2), and that is all the theorem needs.

## Independent audit (20 October 2026)

A separate review of the whole chain returned **sound, with notes**:
- `Snake` is the core definition (`work/final_lean/Snake9.lean`), with no shadowing in any of the 686 modules; `verified_interval_191_241` quantifies over all `m` and is inhabited by the checked 191-edge witness. A test that builds a 299-edge `Snake` from raw fields and derives `False` from the theorem confirms it constrains arbitrary induced paths.
- The only `set_option`s in the 283 new modules are `Elab.async false` and `maxRecDepth`; no `native_decide`, `ofReduceBool`, `implemented_by`, `extern`, `unsafe`, `sorry`, `axiom`, `partial` or `opaque` anywhere; the step commands only build terms and submit them through `addDecl`, so the kernel evaluates every check.
- The clause checker's soundness rules are theorems about the snake's vertex set, `localAlpha` is the single constant used by the conditional theorems, and the shape case split is exhaustive (d3 = d0 or new; for five edges d4 ∈ {new, d0, d1}, with `abcab` excluded by a chord).
- One `def F` per refutation namespace is shared by the unsatisfiability theorem and the clause check.
- Note on provenance: the manifest `outputs/strengthened-241-reproduction-manifest.json` is a resumed run (351 of 606 steps cached by mtime, all source hashes equal to HEAD). A from-scratch run in an empty build directory is planned to remove that caveat.

## 239 unconditional (21 October 2026)

The premise of `upper_239_of_direction_windows` (`AggregateDirectionConditional.lean`) is that no window of two, three or four consecutive internal vertices has a direction shape and lumped counts flagged in the automaton table `forbMask`. It is now a theorem, with the same G1/G2 bridge:
- `D239Defs.lean`: ranges with the lumped count `>= 6` read as `[6, 21]`, the table lemmas `tab2/3/4` (every flagged entry of `forbMask` with symbols `0..6` is covered by a certified range tuple of its shape), and the packaged refutations `Refuted2/3/4`.
- `D239Shape.lean`: the shape split (`012`; `0120`, `0123`; `01203`, `01230`, `01231`, `01234`, with `abcab` a chord), normalisation by a cube automorphism forwards or backwards (`norm2` also handles repeated directions), and `no_w2/no_w3/no_w4`.
- `D239Table.lean` (generated): the 145 range tuples from `milestones/10-window-aggregate/data/direction_certificates.json`, one list per shape, and the kernel-decided coverage of `forbMask`; the shape `01231` is never flagged.
- `D239Lex.lean`, `D239LexG1.lean`: lex-leader symmetry breaking. Permuting the coordinates a normalised window does not use fixes the window and every count, so a snake can be replaced by its lexicographically least image; `check2`/`sound2` extend the G1 checker to the lex-leader clauses and `RefutedL` packages a refuted symmetry-broken window.
- 135 window refutations `DV<shape>_<counts>*` (generated): 80 replay the trimmed committed DRUP proof, 55 (the most expensive) replay a CaDiCaL 1.9.5 proof of the committed CNF plus lex-leader clauses for the adjacent transpositions of the free coordinates. The other 10 windows have CNFs identical (sha256) to ones refuted for 241 and reuse those modules.
- `D239Refs.lean` (generated): every range tuple of every shape is refuted. `UpperBound239.lean`: `Snake9.upper_bound_239 : ∀ {m} (p : Snake m), m ≤ 239` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_239`; `#print axioms`: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

**Generator, not sources.** The 1,813 generated modules are 0.9 GB of Lean, so they are not committed (`work/spectral/stronger/DV*.lean` is git-ignored); the five hand-written `D239*.lean` modules and the small generated `D239Table`, `D239Refs` and `UpperBound239` are. `u239/regen.py WORK work/spectral/stronger --jobs 2` rebuilds all of them from committed data in about 150 s:
- `u239/cases.json`: per window, its shape and ranges, the sha256 of the committed CNF and DRUP (`certificates/direction/<tag>.{cnf,drup}.gz`; 97 from the four-centre run, 48 regenerated by the five-centre run), the kind (`reuse`, `trim`, `sym`) and the sha256 of the LRAT proof and, for `sym`, of the symmetry-broken CNF;
- `trim`: `drat-trim -L` on the committed CNF and DRUP; `sym`: `u239/symsolve.py` (lex-leader clauses, CaDiCaL 1.9.5 through PySAT 1.9.dev16, deterministic, then `drat-trim -L`); every LRAT must match its recorded hash;
- `u239/gen.py` (modules per window), `u239/gen_table.py`, `u239/gen_refs.py`; every written file must match `u239/sources.sha256`. A full run on 21 October reproduced all 1,813 files byte for byte. The 80 `trim` windows were generated before the data definitions became `noncomputable def` and `gen.py` keeps that (`legacy`), so the regenerated sources are exactly the ones whose objects were checked.
- `u239/build_order.txt`: the 1,818 new modules in dependency order; `u239/times.py` summarises a compile log.

Costs (one Lean process, `nice -n 10`, on a machine shared with a four-core certifier):
- G2: 936,056 RUP steps and 58.1 million hints (`trim` 130,662 steps / 9.2 M hints; `sym` 805,394 / 48.9 M), about 3,200 hints per second including the input modules: 668 step modules 14,175 s, 135+38 input modules 4,040 s.
- G1: 564 clause-check modules 9,138 s, plus tables and checks 2,764 s. The lex-leader checks make G1 almost as expensive as G2 for the symmetry-broken windows.
- Largest windows: `d01234_a3_3_4_4` (79,218 steps, 4.5 M hints, G2 1,731 s), `d01234_a3_4_3_4` (4.8 M hints, 1,114 s), `d01234_a4_3_3_4` (111,109 steps, 6.3 M hints, 918 s). Largest modules: `D239Refs` 1,148 s, `UpperBound239` 1,088 s (both mostly loading the 1,800 imported objects, 6.3 GB), then `DV01203_4_3_4_3G1C6` 160 s and `DV01234_2_4_2_LS03` 133 s. Peak anonymous memory 1.05 GB (an input module); the final modules map about 10 GB of objects as page cache, and under a 4.3 GB memory cgroup `UpperBound239` thrashed and was stopped after 37 minutes.
- Total: 1,818 modules, 32,600 s (9.1 h) of compilation.

Reproduction `outputs/strengthened-239-reproduction-manifest.json` (log `outputs/strengthened-239-reproduction.log`): `verify_strengthened.py --core-targets Snake9 PathStructure CubeLocal SquareCounterexample SingletonSquareLemma OptimalityTarget --extra-chains K6R3C7W K6R34C7W --bound239 --seed-objects <u239 build> --build-dir .verification/strengthened-239-resume --jobs 1`, success, 2,424 steps in 29 minutes. **This is a resumed run, not a fresh one:** 604 steps reuse objects of the 241 build directory (cached by mtime, as in the 241 manifest), and the 1,818 new modules are `seeded` (new option `--seed-objects`): their objects were compiled module by module, outside the driver, by plain `lean -o` from the same toolchain and mathlib, from sources whose sha256 the driver checks against the copies it builds from (recorded per step in the manifest); a module is seeded only if none of its imports was compiled in the run. Only `FinalAudit` (1,676 s; it imports `UpperBound239` and prints the axioms of every bound, including `D239.ref_*`, `Snake9.upper_bound_239` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_239`) was run by the driver itself. All 67 audited theorems use only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. A from-scratch run (`--bound239` without `--seed-objects`, after `u239/regen.py`) takes about 9 hours of single-process compilation.

**Result: 191 <= a(9) <= 239, unconditional and Lean-checked.**

Making the next bridge cheaper:
- The final two modules (`D239Refs`, `UpperBound239`, 37 minutes together) only load objects. Importing the 135 `G1Check` modules into `D239Refs` pulls in every step module transitively; packaging each window as a small module that re-exports only `refuted` (or splitting `D239Refs` per shape and keeping `UpperBound239` on the six `ref_*` lemmas) would shrink the import closure of the last steps.
- Symmetry breaking pays for G2: for `d0120_a3_3_4` a CaDiCaL proof without lex clauses trims to 607,276 steps and 31.5 M hints, with them to 52,769 steps and 2.7 M hints. G1 is still a large share (11,902 s against 18,403 s for G2 over all 135 windows). An untested idea: check each lex-leader generator once as a block (as `blocksOK` does for counters) instead of clause by clause in `check2`.
- G2 throughput is about 3,200 hints per second; the cost is dominated by hint count, so a solver configuration or proof trimming that minimises hints (not steps) is the direct lever. The input modules (`Inputs`, 3,666 s) cost a quarter of the step modules; one module per chunk would parallelise them.
- Run the final modules without a tight memory cgroup: their anonymous memory stays under 1 GB, but they map about 10 GB of objects, and a 4.3 GB cgroup made them page-fault bound.
