# Toward an unconditional 244: kernel-checked refutations of the (2,2,3) windows

`upper_244_of_no_two_two_three` is conditional on no snake containing three consecutive internal vertices with local counts 2, 2, 3. Two radius-three window CNFs carry that premise (path vertices `0,1,3,7,6` and `0,1,3,7,15`, centres 1, 3, 7). Their DRAT refutations were checked by drat-trim only.

**G2, the refutation (done).** The repository now proves both CNFs unsatisfiable in Lean, with every unit-propagation step checked by the kernel and only the standard axioms:
- `w223_rep_unsat : WinCNF.unsat W223Rep.F`: 1,629 steps, 148,089 hints, 1,245 used clauses;
- `w223_dis_unsat : WinCNF.unsat W223Dis.F`: 17,101 steps, 926,950 hints, 2,172 used clauses.

Where things are:
- Lean modules: `work/spectral/stronger/WinCNF.lean`, `WinRup.lean`, `WinRupCmd.lean`, `W223Rep*.lean`, `W223Dis*.lean`, `W223.lean`.
- Clause semantics: `WinCNF` (`Lit := Int`, `clauseSat`, `unsat F := ∀ τ, (∀ c ∈ F, clauseSat τ c) → False`).
- Bitmask RUP checker with its soundness proof: `WinRup.rup_sound`.
- `WinRupCmd.rup_step` only builds proof terms and hands them to `addDecl`, so the kernel evaluates every check. A corrupted step is rejected; no `ofReduceBool` or native code is used.
- Each `*Inputs.lean` holds the clauses the proof uses, their indices in the full CNF and their kinds, with SHA-256 hashes of both in its header.
- The reproduction driver checks the 21 modules in about 200 s (`outputs/strengthened-direction-232-reproduction-manifest.json`).
- Tools in this folder:
  - `gen2.py` rebuilds the CNF with `wincert.Encoding` and trims the proof to LRAT with drat-trim `-L`;
  - `export_lean.py` writes the Lean modules;
  - `check_data.py` independently checks that the Lean clauses match the CNF file.

**Normalisation (done).** `work/spectral/stronger/CubePermutation.lean` shows that cube automorphisms map snakes to snakes and keep `pathDir` (mapped by the coordinate permutation), `localAlpha` and the (2,2,3) window. Any window with pairwise distinct directions can be normalised.

**G1 and assembly (done, 20 October 2026).**
- `W223G1Defs/Data/Sound.lean`: a kernel-decidable clause checker `check`, with the soundness lemma `sound : Hyp T S → blocksOK T → check T c → clauseSat (tau T S) c`. The tables in `W223G1Data` (generated by `gendata.py`) are untrusted hints that the kernel re-checks.
- `W223G1CheckRep/Dis.lean`: every used clause passes the checker (`decide +kernel`).
- `W223G1Snake.lean`, `W223G1Inst.lean`: a snake through `0,1,3,7,(6|15)` with counts 2,2,3 satisfies the hypotheses. This includes `localAlpha` equal to the number of free corners off the snake.
- `UpperBound244.lean`:
  - the window-shape case split, the normalisation and the G2 refutations give `W223G1.no_223`;
  - then `Snake9.upper_bound_244 : ∀ {m} (p : Snake m), m ≤ 244` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_244`.
- Reproduction: `outputs/strengthened-244-reproduction-manifest.json`, 353 steps, success, standard axioms only. The G1 modules take about 60 s and G2 about 200 s.

**Result: 191 <= a(9) <= 244, unconditional and Lean-checked.**

Making the next bridge cheaper:
- The final two modules (`D239Refs`, `UpperBound239`, 37 minutes together) only load objects. Importing the 135 `G1Check` modules into `D239Refs` pulls in every step module transitively; packaging each window as a small module that re-exports only `refuted` (or splitting `D239Refs` per shape and keeping `UpperBound239` on the six `ref_*` lemmas) would shrink the import closure of the last steps.
- Symmetry breaking pays for G2: for `d0120_a3_3_4` a CaDiCaL proof without lex clauses trims to 607,276 steps and 31.5 M hints, with them to 52,769 steps and 2.7 M hints. G1 is still a large share (11,902 s against 18,403 s for G2 over all 135 windows). An untested idea: check each lex-leader generator once as a block (as `blocksOK` does for counters) instead of clause by clause in `check2`.
- G2 throughput is about 3,200 hints per second; the cost is dominated by hint count, so a solver configuration or proof trimming that minimises hints (not steps) is the direct lever. The input modules (`Inputs`, 3,666 s) cost a quarter of the step modules; one module per chunk would parallelise them.
- Run the final modules without a tight memory cgroup: their anonymous memory stays under 1 GB, but they map about 10 GB of objects, and a 4.3 GB cgroup made them page-fault bound.

## Toward 235: shape-level bridge (u235/, 22-23 October 2026)

`upper_235_of_direction_windowsK6R3W` (AggregateDirectionK6R3WConditional) assumes that no window of 2..6 consecutive internal vertices has a direction shape and lumped counts flagged in the K6R3W table. The table holds 2,635 certified exclusions: 34 shapes, radius 3, ranges exact `1..5` or lumped `[6, 21]`.

The new bridge works per shape, not per window. It discharges the premise completely in Lean, conditional only on per-shape refutations, and it currently bridges 19 of the 34 shapes.

**Design**
- **One shape CNF per shape** (`gen235.py`). It contains:
  - the wincert ball, endpoint and cycle-cut clauses, with no count constraint;
  - one totalizer per centre over its 21 absent-corner literals, whose outputs mean "α ≥ j";
  - lex-leader clauses for the adjacent transpositions of the free coordinates.
- **One CaDiCaL 2.1.3 session per shape** (`--lidrup`, the binary shipped with the Lean 4.19.0 toolchain, deterministic):
  - one assumption cube per window, `o_lo` and `¬o_(hi+1)`;
  - the proof is trimmed backwards from all window targets (`mtrim.c`).
- **G1, once per shape** (`Sh235G1.lean`):
  - `check3` accepts the base and lex clauses (`check2`) and the four totalizer clause families. They are read through `wire`: a literal is a counting statement `j ≤ cnt L` over a node's leaf list.
  - `sound3` proves every accepted clause true under the intended assignment `tau3`.
- **G2** (`FastRupT.lean`): FastRup blocks export the target clauses. `fr_tgt` turns each export into `∀ τ, Inp τ → clauseSat τ C` in the original numbering, by the converse of `msat_toMaskR`.
- **Window link** (`Sh235Win.lean`, `winRef_of`):
  1. A snake through the canonical window is first moved to its lex-least image, which keeps the window and every count (`D239.lexmin`).
  2. The window's totalizer roots count exactly the absent free corners (`cenOK`, `CornerOK`, `center_counts`).
  3. The target `C = ¬core` is false under the window's counts (`litOff`, decided by the kernel), which is a contradiction.
- **Normalisation, generic in the shape** (`Win235Norm.lean`):
  - `norm_canon` sends the `i`-th distinct direction of any window to `i` by a cube automorphism, which is exactly `canon`.
  - `revS` reads the snake backwards; its counts and directions are reversed.
- **Table link** (`Win235Glue.lean`, generated `D235Data`, `D235Tab_*`, `D235Table`):
  - `rgsOK`: every restricted growth string of length `n + 1` is listed, or it and its reversal have shape index 34, whose tables vanish (`sfwd_big`, `srev_big`).
  - `TabOK` (51 listed direction lists, checked in pieces of 1,296 symbol tuples cut out of the table): every flagged tuple with symbols `1..6` is covered by a range tuple of the shape read forwards, or of its reversal read backwards.
  - `no_win` combines these with the normalisation. `S235.T235.prem2..prem6` are the five premises in exactly the form of the conditional theorem, and `upper_235_of_shapes` gives 235 from `∀ e ∈ E, ShapeRefuted e.c e.L`.
- **Cap-5 lumping and positivity:** the symbol `6` (`sym a = 6` iff `a ≥ 6`) is covered only by ranges `[≤ 6, ≥ 21]`. Internal counts are at least 1 (`localAlpha_positive`), so symbol `0` never occurs.
- **Reversed patterns:** the reverse table `srev` is checked directly against the reversal `canon c.reverse`, read backwards on `revS`.

**Status**
- 19 shapes are bridged, 426 of the 2,635 windows (`Q<dirs>Ref`).
- `UpperBound235Cond.lean`, `S235.T235.upper_235_of_remaining`, proves the 235 bound from the refutation of the other 15 shapes alone, with standard axioms only.
- Session statistics for all 34 shapes are in `u235/sessions235.json`: in total 4.31e8 hints after trimming, at 61 hints per lemma.

| | bridged 19 shapes | all 34 shapes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hints | 30.5 M | 431 M |
| G2 time (FastRup, about 8,300 hints/s per module) | 3,674 module-s | about 52,000 s (14.4 h) |
| G1 time (once per shape) | 1,374 s | about 2,500 s |
| objects (about 295 bytes per hint) | 9.0 GB | about 127 GB |

**Why the remaining 15 shapes are not bridged here.** The obstacle is object size, not kernel time:
- A FastRup hint is about six `Expr.app` nodes in the stored proof term. The 431 M hints of the full set would need about 127 GB of `.olean`, and all of them must be present when the final module is imported. This machine has 15 GB.
- A reflective checker with packed hint data would be small, about 3 to 25 bytes per hint. But measured kernel loops cost 10 to 20 µs per iteration, recursion depth is limited to about 5,000 per evaluation, and random access into a clause store costs about 20 µs per binary tree level. That puts it at roughly 2 to 5 times the FastRup time per hint.
- Two levers remain: a block-level clause tree of `(mask, proof)` pairs with packed hint indices (estimated 10 to 30 bytes and about 110 µs per hint), or about 10 times fewer hints.

**Reproduction**
- `u235/regen235.py WORK work/spectral/stronger` rewrites the generated sources from committed data and checks them against `u235/sources.sha256`. The committed data are `cuts235.json` (the cycle cuts of every shape) and `shapes_done.txt`.
- Then run `verify_strengthened.py ... --bound235`. The modules are listed in `u235/build_order.txt`.
- Peak anonymous memory per module is at most 3.0 GB: the window theorems come in groups of 60 per module, and the RGS sweep tests the shape index before list membership.

## Toward 234: share bridge (u234/, 23 October 2026)

The K6R3W chain proves `4 m ≤ Σ α + 28` from the 235 window premises. With a share lower bound of 21 on every six-centre window, `ShareBonus.upper_of_walk_and_shares` gives `4935 m ≤ 1159221`, so `m ≤ 234` (234.89). The wrapper is `AggregateDirectionK6R3WShare234.lean`, generated by `gen_share_wrapper.py --suffix K6R3W --L 7 --k 6 --K 5 --lam 4/1 --C 28 --shapes 34 --lb 21 --bound 234`. This bridge proves the share premise in Lean: for every snake and every window `j` with `1 ≤ j` and `j + 7 ≤ m`, `21 ≤ windowShareK p 6 j` (`SH234.shares_K6R3W`, no hypotheses).

**Certificates.** There is one share CNF per six-centre shape: the committed certificate CNF of `milestones/10-window-aggregate/data/share/final_a_X20.jsonl`. `gen234.py` regenerates it with `ShareEncoding` and checks its sha256 against the recorded `cnf_sha256`. The 34 certificate shapes are exactly `shapesK6R3W`, all canonical seven-direction shapes, so no reversed shapes are needed. The CNFs contain no lex-leader clauses, as checked: every clause falls into one of the base, `E`, `g` or totalizer families. Each CNF gets one CaDiCaL 2.1.3 `--lrat` proof, trimmed backwards from the empty clause and checked by CRup (G2: `V<dirs>.unsatF : unsat F`).

**G1** (`ShareG1.lean`, `check4` / `sound4`):
- The intended assignment `tauS` sets `E` true iff the square `Q` is empty, and `g` true iff `E` holds and at most `t` of the free corners `pv` are selected. For `g1`, `t = 1 - nfix`; for `g3`, `t = 0`. A totalizer output `j` of a node is true iff the term values of the node's range sum to at least `j`.
- The rule families:
  - base clauses, all variables below `VS`, checked by `W223G1.check`;
  - `[¬E, ¬x]` and `[E, x₁..x₄]`;
  - `[¬g, E]`;
  - `[¬g, ¬x₁..¬x_{t+1}]`, with the corners a sublist of `pv`;
  - `[¬E, x.., g]`, where `|pv| ≤ #x + t`;
  - totalizer up clauses `[¬A_i, ¬B_j, O_k]` and `[¬A_i, O_k]` with `k ≤ i + j`, where every node has consecutive term ranges `[lo, mid)` and `[mid, hi)` and a term child is read through its bits;
  - the bound units `¬O_21` on nodes whose range starts at term 0, which are sound when the term values of `[0, NT)` sum to at most 20.
- A term's value is the largest index of a true bit (`topv`). A true bit at index `i` therefore certifies a value of at least `i`, and the soundness proof does not depend on any monotonicity of the bits.
- All tables are packed into fixed-width fields of chunks of 64 big numbers (`tget`), which keeps the kernel's caches small. A first version that used tree-shaped data terms took more than 10 minutes to elaborate. The modules set `Elab.async false`; without it the G1 check modules ran their kernel checks in parallel and needed up to 2.8 GB.

**Link to `share`** (`ShareWin.lean`):
- `termOK` checks, per term, that:
  - its `E` square contains the four vertices of `Q = x ^ e_k + span(e_a, e_b)`, with `x = wv c (cen + 1)`;
  - `k ∉ {a, b}`;
  - each bit at index `i` is `E` (`i < 2`) or a `g` whose `pv` is a sublist of the other three corners of `P`, with `i + 1 ≤ gBound pv t` (6 if `pv` is all three corners and `t = 0`; 3 if `t + 2 ≤ |pv|`; otherwise 2).
- `tval_le` proves that the term value is at most `shareTerm q k {a, b} x`. Here `sqCount = 1 + #selected(P \ x)`, which is at most 3 by `no_full_square`, and `shareWeight` is 6/3/2.
- The keys `(cen, k, a, b)` are strictly increasing, so the term values sum to at most `Σ_{centre} Σ_k Σ_ab shareTerm = windowShareK q 6 r` (`rsum_le_windowShare`). Path directions `k` and pairs containing `k` contribute nothing extra, because all terms are bounded by the full `vertexShare` sums.
- `shareRef_of` turns a refuted share CNF into `ShareRef c 21`: every snake through the window `wv c` has share sum at least 21.

**Normalisation** (`ShareNorm.lean`):
- `share_cubeMap` shows that shares are invariant under every cube automorphism, via translations (`shareTerm_translate`) and coordinate permutations with the pair re-sorted (`shareTerm_permSnake`).
- `norm_canon_share` is `S235.norm_canon` with `shareSeq` kept as well.
- `shareRef_window` and `window_shape_memK6R3W` (kernel RGS coverage) give `shares_K6R3W` in `ShareRefs234.lean`.

**Assembly** (`UpperBound234Cond.lean`):
- `S235.T235.upper_234_of_shapes` derives 234 from `∀ e ∈ E, ShapeRefuted e.c e.L`, through `prem2..prem6` of `D235Table`.
- `upper_234_of_remaining` has exactly the hypotheses of `upper_235_of_remaining`. `gen_final234.py --cond` reads them from `UpperBound235Cond.lean`.
- When the 235 bridge exports the full `∀ e ∈ E, …`, `gen_final234.py --uncond NAME --uncond-module M` writes `UpperBound234.lean` with `Snake9.upper_bound_234` and `Snake9.verified_interval_191_234`.
