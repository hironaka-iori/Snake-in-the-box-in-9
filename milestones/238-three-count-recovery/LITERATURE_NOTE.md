# Bounded literature check

On 18 November 2026, searches for hypercube adjacency inversion and reconstruction from neighbour sums located related primary literature. This was a bounded search, not an exhaustive novelty investigation.

- Chandler, Sin and Xiang, [The Smith group of the hypercube graph](https://cpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/sites.udel.edu/dist/a/6387/files/2017/08/csx_hypercube-vm2v4a.pdf), DOI 10.1007/s10623-016-0291-7. The paper computes the elementary divisors of the adjacency matrix. Its integer-lattice viewpoint is relevant to the divisibility restrictions in the present calculation. The inspected introduction does not establish the present bounded-selection erasure statement.
- Pebody, [Set Reconstruction on the Hypercube](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.05675). The abstract concerns reconstruction from translated small-subset decks. That observation model differs from labelled neighbour counts.
- Alasmari and Aliev, [On unique recovery of finite-valued integer signals and admissible lattices of sparse hypercubes](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11590-022-01927-0). The abstract and introductory formulation concern constructing measurement matrices for sparse bounded integer signals. The present matrix is fixed by Q9 adjacency.

No claim of priority is made for adjacency inversion or the three-erasure consequence. The new round supplies a self-contained argument and checked Q9 specialization for this research archive. A broader literature review remains necessary before claiming novelty.
