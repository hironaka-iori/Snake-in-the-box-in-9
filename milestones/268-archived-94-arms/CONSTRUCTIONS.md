# Placing and rebuilding the opposite arm

Of the 329 archived examples with a 94-edge and a 95-edge arm, 35 use one of the five shorter-arm shapes already covered in Round 266. The other 294 give the 63 shorter-arm shapes examined here. Their published partners give 190 edges after adding the crossing. None of these 294 partners admits a one-edge addition at its free tip with the parent fixed. To reach 192, a replacement partner must have 97 edges. Keeping the crossing fixed determines its translation, leaving a permutation of the eight coordinates.

The Round 267 collection supplied fourteen distinct ordered 97-edge transition sequences up to renaming coordinates. These include every such segment in either direction in the collection of 2,760 shapes. At each of the 126 fixed endpoints, the calculation tried all 40,320 permutations of every ordered segment. Across all 71,124,480 placements, the smallest number of common projected vertices in addition to the crossing was eight. Two published endpoints attain eight. In the saved coordinates their additional common sites are (7,18,55,56,98,124,236,255). The minimum at individual endpoints ranges from eight to eighteen.

Each such site would connect nonconsecutive vertices on the two opposite faces. Keeping the fixed labelled parent and a donor in that placement requires removing every one of these sites from the donor to obtain a legal partner. The count is specific to this collection of candidates. The unrestricted partner search is recorded in the [main result](README.md).

For each donor and fixed endpoint, the first computation added the obstruction indicators for all permutations in parallel. Minimising the resulting binary counts gave a placement attaining the reported minimum. An independent recurrence counted permutations with fewer intersections, reading the donor vertices backwards. Every subminimum count was zero.

Shortening candidate partners also gave valid lower bounds. At the reverse ends the best starting placement had 158 joined edges. Rebuilding just the opposite arm at eight selected reverse ends raised the strongest explicit join to 182. The eight resulting joins have lengths 170, 172, 173, 176, 179, 180, 181 and 182. Their full vertex sequences were checked for all pairwise adjacencies. The best across both orientations remained 190.
