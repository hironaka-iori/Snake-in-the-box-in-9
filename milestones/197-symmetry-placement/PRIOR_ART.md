# Symmetry construction precedents

Read on13 November2026. No novelty claim is made for symmetry, orbit quotients, involution classification, or path reversal.

Ed Wynn, *Constructing circuit codes by permuting initial sequences* (2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.1647. The abstract describes permuted copies of an initial transition sequence and joins of two lower-dimensional paths after permuting the second to fit the first. The abstract was inspected in this round; no additional theorem from the full text is used here.

Tom Taylor, *Constructing longer snakes and improved asymptotic bounds in hypercubes* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27483. The abstract describes compatible paths with controlled overlaps joined across cube layers. This is already cited in the manuscript. The abstract was refreshed in this round; its higher-dimensional numerical bounds are not used in the Q9 deduction.

The current contribution is an exact symmetry-placement census relative to one saved Q7 path, a concrete checked quotient description, and restricted repair outcomes. This does not classify all snakes or prove a new global bound.
