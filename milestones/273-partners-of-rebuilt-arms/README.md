## The construction

Round 272 supplies 84 inequivalent 96-edge snakes in $Q_8$. Eighty-three are absent as whole shapes from the earlier donor collections. Each has two distinct choices of crossing endpoint. Place the chosen endpoint at zero, and write its ordered arm $A$ starting at zero. Any induced opposite arm $B$ starting at zero fits precisely when the two vertex sets intersect only at zero. Reverse $A$, take the edge from 0 to 256, and follow $256+B$. The join has $96+1+|E(B)|$ edges. A 192-edge join requires a 95-edge opposite arm. The placement catalogue contains 3,649 eight-dimensional donor shapes. Enumerating the available directed segments gives 5,074 different rooted transition words of length 95. Neither donor reversal nor a change in the coordinate names needs to preserve the labels of the fixed parent. Trying all $8!=40{,}320$ permutations for every pattern and crossing gives 34,370,058,240 placements. All have an unwanted cross-face contact. The best shorter placement joins 187 edges. The Boolean formula selects the sites of a partner and enforces the bipartition counts and vertex degrees. A disconnected cycle found during search is cut from the next formula. A contradiction excludes each parent in its group. Rebuilding the formula and checking its LRAT certificate independently verifies a contradiction. For a satisfying assignment the ordered geometric path is restored to the original coordinates and every unordered pair is checked. ## Projected donor conflicts

For each crossing, a separate comparison uses the long arms of record constructions, the historical 98-edge donors and the longer partners constructed in Round 271. In each direction, retain at most the first 95 edges. The shortlist includes words of at least 91 edges. For a directed donor of $b$ edges with $h$ forbidden nonzero sites after placement, a compatible 95-edge partner requires at least $95-b+h$ sites outside that donor. The minimum over the shortlist lies between eight and sixteen depending on the crossing. Two endpoints attain the value eight. The count was checked independently by propagating the coordinate permutations that encounter at most a prescribed number of forbidden sites.
