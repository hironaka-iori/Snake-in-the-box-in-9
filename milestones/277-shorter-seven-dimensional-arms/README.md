# Round 277 · A 48-edge arm in seven dimensions

**The maximum is 96 edges for an eight-dimensional snake that crosses a coordinate once and has a 48-edge arm which itself crosses some coordinate once.** Exactly 11 eight-dimensional shapes attain 96 in this family. Five choices of a seven-dimensional crossing endpoint produce them. No 48- or 49-edge opposite arm fits. [Ordered eight-dimensional paths](q8-arms.json) · [Counts and source hashes](summary.json)

## The remaining shape of a possible 98

If an eight-dimensional snake of 98 edges crosses one coordinate exactly once, its two seven-dimensional arms have lengths adding to 97. The published seven-dimensional maximum is 50, leaving only the unordered possibilities $(50,47)$ and $(49,48)$. Round 270 treated both endpoints of every one of Palani's six optimal undirected 50-edge shapes. None accepts a 47-edge opposite arm. This first exclusion depends on Palani's classification of all optimal seven-dimensional shapes. [Palani, Tables 5.1–5.2](https://ai.uga.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/theses/palani_ananta.pdf#page=54) · [Complete endpoint census](../270-optimal-seven-dimensional-arms/README.md)

In the remaining $(49,48)$ case, Round 272 excludes **every 49-edge arm with an internally once-crossed coordinate**, with the other side unrestricted. Round 277 excludes **every 48-edge arm with an internally once-crossed coordinate**, again with the other side unrestricted. Both arms must use all seven coordinates, since the maximum in six dimensions is 26. Consequently, in a 98-edge snake of this kind, each of the seven remaining coordinates must occur at least twice in **each** arm, hence at least four times in the entire path. Round 272 also shows that the 49-edge arm can contain no 20-edge interval of an optimal seven-dimensional snake up to cube isometry and reversal. Whether a 98-edge path exists subject to all these conditions remains open. [49-edge census and six-dimensional bound](../272-forty-nine-edge-parents/README.md) · [48-edge census](summary.json)

## Eight-dimensional constructions

The direct constructions have the split $48+1+47=96$. There are 570 unrooted 48-edge seven-dimensional paths with an internal once-used coordinate, giving 1,140 choices of oriented crossing endpoint. Grouping by shared forbidden vertices gives 382 searches. Only nine labelled opposite paths reach 47 edges in these enlarged groups, and none reaches 48. Filtering each against the particular roots gives the 11 actual joins. Their canonical transition words are all distinct. [Geometric argument, grouping and independent algorithms](METHOD.md) · [Primary traversal](mask-groups.json) · [Independent replay](contacts-groups.json)

Eight of the 11 shapes were already present in the Round 267 catalogue. The other three are absent as whole paths and as intervals of all 3,864 named earlier shapes in the comparison. They are also absent from every 96-edge interval across the 1,042 archived 190-edge and 131 archived 191-edge nine-dimensional snakes. All three arise by joining a single 47-edge opposite path to three different rooted 48-edge parents. [Three new ordered arms](q8-new.json) · [Archive matches for the complete catalogue](archive-occurrences.json)

As a second construction, keep all but at most two original vertices of each of the eleven 96-edge paths and freely reconnect the remaining pieces with new vertices in the eight-dimensional cube. Enumerating all choices of zero, one and two omitted vertices yields no 97- or 98-edge path. Consequently any such longer path must omit at least three sites of each direct donor in every placement. This search permits changes to the old crossing. The supplementary 69 length-95 constructions come from trimming a 49- or 50-edge side of earlier ordered paths to 48 while retaining its 46-edge partner. Rebuilding at these 69 shapes with at most one omission gives exactly 17 different 97-edge paths, all in the earlier catalogue. ## Reproduction

Use the [guarded runner](../256-two-exchange-parents/run.py) with `enumerate_groups.py mask`, `enumerate_groups.py contacts`, and `summarize.py`, in that order. The optional `--count` argument limits a traversal to that many pending groups and permits resumption. Every row in the independent replay must match the entire sorted primary list of relaxed opposite paths before either output is filtered by the individual parent. A vertex sequence is finally checked by comparing adjacency against consecutiveness for every pair.

The additional reconstruction uses `grow_nearby.py --mode single` and `grow_nearby.py --mode double` on the eleven direct paths, then `shorter_witnesses.py` followed by `grow_nearby.py --source shortened --mode single`. Removed seed sites are forbidden from reappearing. The remaining ordered fragments can be permuted and reversed, with new sites in their gaps or at either free end. This accounts for every path containing the specified retained sites and omitting exactly the selected sites. [Fragment enumeration](../274-rebuilding-near-a-fixed-arm/near.py)

The keys of [q8-arms.json](q8-arms.json) and [q8-new.json](q8-new.json) are canonical hexadecimal transition words. Each record supplies the ordered eight-dimensional vertex sequence in `joined_q8`, the seven-dimensional arms in `parent48` and `partner`, and the archive comparisons. Both seven-dimensional lists start at their projected crossing. A direct 96-edge construction needs a compatible 95-edge eight-dimensional opposite arm to reach 192 in nine dimensions.
