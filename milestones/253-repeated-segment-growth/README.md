# Round 253 · Repeated changes retaining 68 edges

A change keeps 68 consecutive edges of an induced path in the eight-dimensional cube and regrows both ends to a length of 96, 97 or 98 edges. Either added end can have length zero. A different segment can be retained at the next change. Paths related by a translation, a coordinate permutation or reversal count as one shape.

Starting from the 22 archived references, any finite number of changes reaches exactly **237 shapes**. Of these, 229 have 96 edges, seven have 97 and one has 98. The 174 beyond the one-change catalogue all have 96 edges. Every possible next change of every one of the 237 shapes was included. ## Why arbitrarily many changes are covered

For each newly reached shape the enumeration records every 68-edge window up to isometry and reversal. Each previously unseen window is extended freely at both ends. This process exhausted 2,269 distinct windows and visited 35,136,720 right-tail search nodes. The numbers of new shapes at minimum distances zero through nine from the 22 references were 22, 41, 22, 15, 15, 34, 52, 21, 10 and 5. Expanding the last five found no new shape. Induction on the number of moves gives completeness, and each recorded discovery supplies a path back to a reference. [Search program](closure.py) · [Independent two-tail replay](replay.py)

The second enumerator grew the two tails separately and rejected every pair that overlapped or touched away from the retained segment. It reproduced the same 237 shapes and every distance layer. Lean checked the general argument transferring a closed catalogue with decreasing predecessor ranks into exact reachability, and the implication from bounds on all terminal shapes to bounds on their joins.
