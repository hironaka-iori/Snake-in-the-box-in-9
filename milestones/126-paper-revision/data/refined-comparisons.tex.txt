\subsection{Later restrictions and replacement cycles}
A further proposed rule for $012034$ is
\[
\alpha_{r+3}\le5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\alpha_{r+1}+\alpha_{r+2}+\alpha_{r+4}+\alpha_{r+5}\ge16.
\]
An independent native replay verifies its contradiction, but the complete
count interpretation and Lean replay remain pending. Granting this rule
raises the first comparison mean to $2915/6$, with range $1730/3$; the
conditional-share mean and range remain 496 and 602. The corresponding
length fractions are
\[
\frac{1098960\cdot6+8\cdot2915+3460}{4200\cdot6+2915}
=\frac{1324108}{5623},\qquad \frac{551765}{2348}.
\]
Their integer comparisons are still 235 and 234~\cite{latersumarchive}.

Subsequent tests identify limits that are relevant to both profiles.
On $012301$, proposed outer-four bound 13 fails in the relaxed local model:
checked disconnected forests have count tuples $(4,2,10,2,3)$ and
$(4,2,10,4,2)$. No complete-snake realization is inferred. On $012304$, a
checked complete 148-edge snake has counts $(3,6,3,4,6)$, so a five-count
bound 23 fails even with first, third and fourth counts at most four and
the other two at least six. The path and exact counts have passed Lean.
Two more specific five-count restrictions on $012304$ have independently
replayed negative certificates, with their complete Lean deductions still
pending~\cite{latersumarchive}.

The proved $012031$ restriction in Proposition~\ref{prop:outer-012031} and
the sharp $012032$ pair theorem remove additional transitions, but
replacement cycles preserve both displayed means and ranges. Even deleting
every endpoint-eligible equality key and every key with six recorded counts
equal to four leaves attaining cycles. These deletions are hypothetical
where their local exclusions remain unproved. In particular, the surviving
three-cycle after the endpoint deletion has no eligible equality window;
additional credit confined to such windows cannot increase its mean.

The latest combined experiment grants six-count floors 26, 25 and 25 on
$0123042$, $0120310$ and $0123024$, respectively, in addition to the inherited
rules. Each of these three local floors remains unproved. A symbol below
six specifies an exact count; symbol six retains its full interval
$[6,21]$. The overlap accounting uses at most coefficient 20 for unrestricted
six-position windows. Coefficient 24 is valid under the tested guard that
the first count is exact and below six, since its excess is zero; overlapping
alternative credits use their maximum. Thus no second use of the same
excess is granted~\cite{latersumarchive,uniformsixarchive}.

This experiment removes 10082 additional keys and increases credit on
41762 retained keys. All 4673032 retained-edge inequalities were checked in
the producer's exact enumeration, with an independent audit of the saved
cycles, potentials and arithmetic. Both attaining cycles avoid the three
tested patterns, so their means remain $2915/6$ and 496. Any further
strengthening restricted to those three shapes leaves these cycle ceilings
unchanged. Even setting the potential ranges to zero gives
\[
\frac{1323416}{5623}>235,\qquad
\frac{137866}{587}>234.
\]
Hence range reduction alone cannot improve the respective integer bounds.
The conditional four-cycle contains, up to reversal, $0120324$ windows with
exact six-count sums 22, 24 and 26. A proved floor 23 would exclude its
sum-22 edge; Section~\ref{sec:critical-six} records the remaining difficulty.
These are comparison-graph cycles, with no asserted realization by complete
snakes~\cite{uniformsixarchive,criticalsixarchive}.
