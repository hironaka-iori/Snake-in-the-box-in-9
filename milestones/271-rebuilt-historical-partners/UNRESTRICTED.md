# Rebuilding the opposite arm over its entire face

The 54 eight-dimensional parent shapes from Round 270 have 108 choices of crossing endpoint. There are 18 shapes of length 97 and 36 of length 96. A nine-dimensional snake of length 192 requires an opposite arm of 94 or 95 edges respectively. The crossing and the entire parent stay fixed. Every induced path from zero that avoids the parent's other projected vertices is allowed. [Parent vertices and initial constructions](../270-optimal-seven-dimensional-arms/opposite-constructions.json)

All **108 rooted parents exclude 192 edges**. Thirty-four whole-group contradictions cover 107 endpoints. At the remaining Bitterman D2-18 endpoint, the crossing admits seven first steps. Three are excluded directly, and sixteen contradictions cover every second step after the other four. Thus 53 contradictions suffice at target 192. At target 189, eight whole-group contradictions give a stronger ceiling of 188 for 15 endpoints. [Target 192](queries-192.json) · [Target 189](queries-189.json) · [Independent replay](summary.json)

The calculation groups parents of the same length under permutations of the eight coordinates. Selecting a particular parent forbids all its nonzero sites. A contradiction for one group excludes the target at each of its members. A satisfying selection is ordered as a path, joined to a compatible parent, and transported back to that parent's original coordinates. Degree sums on the two parity classes determine the free endpoint. Any disconnected cycles found during the search are excluded. The same 92-edge partner fits both of the 96-edge parents in group 26 after their recorded coordinate changes. The intervening crossing brings each joined path to 189 edges. Both parents had a 133-edge join recorded at this crossing in Round 270. The independent reconstruction gives the original coordinate sequences, checks all 17,955 unordered vertex pairs for each path and compares the transition words up to translation, coordinate permutation and reversal. The two 189-edge words are inequivalent. Neither path can gain one edge at either end in the nine-dimensional cube. Their words differ from every length-189 interval of the 1,042 archived 190-edge paths and 131 bank 191-edge paths, comprising 2,477 intervals before deduplication. This is a comparison with the specified collection.

A contradiction at joined length 190 covers every opposite arm for these two fixed parents at these crossings. A longer arm would supply the excluded length by deleting edges from its free end. Consequently **189 is the exact maximum at each of these two crossings**, with a maximum of 92 edges in the opposite arm. The 92-edge donor was subsequently placed at every one of the 108 fixed crossings, using either donor endpoint and all $8!$ coordinate permutations. The 8,709,120 placements contain exactly two complete fits, one for each original 189-edge join and both with the original donor endpoint. For every other root, even the longest fitting prefix of either rooted donor gave at most 121 joined edges. Independent occupied-set comparisons confirmed every complete-fit count. Earlier certificates supply 557 rooted forbidden sets, counted with their source identifiers. If a permutation puts one of those sets inside the nonzero sites of a new parent, its bound on the opposite arm transfers. The scan found no such inclusion for these 108 roots. The search therefore requires direct certificates. [Source digests and transport results](transfers.json) · [Inclusion calculation](transport271.py)

After prescribing a prefix in the opposite arm, remove its used vertices except its tip and all neighbours of the earlier vertices except the tip. Translate the tip to zero, retaining the forbidden parent vertices. This gives an equivalent rooted path problem with fewer remaining edges. An exhaustive exclusion across the allowed prefixes implies a whole-root exclusion. In the independent replay every recorded formula is rebuilt, each final contradiction is checked against the exact clauses, and the available next steps are reconstructed from cube distances. A search reaching its time limit supplies no exclusion.

To allow a named parent to occur strictly inside a longer arm, enumerate every single legal extension of either end in the eight-dimensional face. Repeating these extensions lists all containing arm shapes. Taking the largest endpoint ceiling among those shapes bounds every one-crossing snake having the specified parent as a consecutive interval in either arm. ## Rebuilding the free end of an existing partner

For the 20 rooted parents whose Round 270 joins had at least 178 edges, the free-tail search starts from 23 available partners. These include the two 188-edge constructions. Keep a crossing-side prefix of the partner and enumerate all ways to continue from it while avoiding the parent. Freeing the last 12, 20 or 28 edges gave no longer partner for any of the 23 starts. Freeing 36 edges gave complete enumerations for 20 starts and exhausted the search allowance for three. There was no improvement among the visited paths.

The search uses a bit set of available vertices and an upper bound from the two available parity counts. The replay of complete cases instead maintains the number of occupied neighbours at each vertex. A new next vertex must have exactly one occupied neighbour, the current tip. Exhausting that recursion gives the maximum length for the stated retained prefix.
