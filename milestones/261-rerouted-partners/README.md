# Round 261 · New partner constructions at the 42 endpoints

Explicit constructions improved the previously recorded Round 257 join at 27 of the 42 endpoints. The resulting lengths range from 166 to 178 edges. At 28 endpoints the new partner begins with a 51-edge transition sequence outside all 2,074 earlier rooted donor sequences. The largest join in this search has 178 edges, a length already attained in Round 257. The largest increase at one endpoint was from 128 to 175 edges. All 15,400 vertex pairs of the new join were checked. [Explicit 175-edge construction](improved-join.json)

The construction started from the earlier best path at each endpoint. It replaced interior intervals, changed obstructing vertices and regrew the free end. Each resulting path was checked for distinct vertices, consecutive adjacency and absence of every nonconsecutive adjacency. The 42 resulting partners, the earlier distant-shell partners and the Round 255 constructed partners gave 55 distinct donor shapes. At each root we placed every directed segment of each shape in all coordinate permutations, selected one longest compatible placement, and verified that a segment one edge longer could not fit. Transfer between roots improved six of the regrown paths. [Initial regrowth](growth.json) · [Transfer and interval search](intervals.py)

For each of the 42 selected seeds, we fixed the parent, the crossing, the two ends of an interval, and all of the partner outside that interval. The old interval had between two and eight edges. We allowed an induced replacement up to six edges longer. Bipartiteness requires an even change in length. None of the 21,112 intervals admitted a longer replacement under these conditions. A second search traversed each interval in reverse with direct checks against all previously selected neighbours and recovered the same maximum. This excludes those particular edits of the selected paths. Longer intervals, simultaneous edits and changing an endpoint remain possible.
