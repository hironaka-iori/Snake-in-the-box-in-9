# Rebuilding the opposite arm in the nine-cube

The strongest construction found here keeps a 96-edge eight-dimensional parent at the same crossing endpoint. Its longest compatible placement from the chosen donor collection gave a nine-dimensional snake of 144 edges. Regrowing the opposite arm produced a 90-edge partner and a 187-edge snake. The file contains both arms and all 188 vertices in order. The search verified the path by pairwise distances and independently checked that each vertex has precisely its predecessor and successor as neighbours among the occupied sites, with the single appropriate neighbour at either endpoint. [187-edge construction](best-187.json) · [All constructions](opposite-constructions.json)

The 53 shapes in the seven-dimensional enumeration and one additional 97-edge historical shape give 54 eight-dimensional parents. Each has two distinct rooted versions, so the construction considered 108 possible crossing endpoints. To place a donor, translate its first site and the chosen parent endpoint to zero. The remaining donor vertices must avoid every nonzero parent vertex. A common projected vertex other than zero would introduce an extra cross-face edge.

The search used 3,555 donor shapes and 54,520 directed segments of lengths 84 through 98. At the 97-edge parents there were 8,124 directed target sequences of length 94. At the 96-edge parents there were 4,818 of length 95. For each rooted parent the search considered all 40,320 permutations of the eight axes. A second scan reversed the order in which it checked the projected sites, confirming the absence of a 192-edge placement in that collection. Twenty endpoints were selected for regrowth, including the two primary-source controls. Their original 182- and 186-edge joins were independently recovered.
