# Two partial Q8 column projections of a Q9 snake

For axis a define a column by zeroing bit a. The two vertices above a column are independent occupancy variables on the original 512-vertex Q9. Each designated occupied column imposes their disjunction. Neither layer, the order of visits, nor endpoints are given. Vertices that have no legal label under two simultaneous negative column constraints can be removed. In the positive-only runs every one of the 512 Q9 vertices remains eligible and no vertex is preselected.

The input is the frozen 131-row bank q9_port_followup_public191.txt.gz in the parent directory. Its earlier 1,043 distinct complete single-axis sets already have a 191-edge upper bound (Q9_PROJECTION_BOUND.md). The present experiments change the projection restrictions.

* Four partial-mask experiments per mother release random shared Q7 cells, independent sets of columns, neighborhoods of scattered mother-path vertices, or complementary halves of Q7. Each projection must differ from the mother on at least one released column. Of 524 cases, MiniCard returned UNSAT on 493 and reached its conflict budget on 31. The final retry budget was 85,000 conflicts.
* In 262 positive-only cases, the constraints retain all but 0, 2, 5, 9, 16 or 24 occupied columns of each source projection. All initially empty columns may enter. There were 131 same-source and 131 cross-source inputs. Each half gave 110 UNSAT and 21 unresolved. The final retry budget was 14,000 conflicts.
* The eight prespecified zero-based rows 0, 13, 37, 58, 71, 94, 113 and 130 were also tested on every unordered pair of axes. Requiring every original column of both projections to remain present still allows additions anywhere. The exact-193-vertex run returned UNSAT on every one of the 288 input pairs. That batch requests a change in each projection, since unchanged full projections were previously ruled out. For comparison, allpairs_atleast.py removes that change requirement, allows any number of vertices from 193 upwards, and omits endpoint same-parity restrictions. independent_cells.py independently implements a direct CNF relaxation of the same larger problem, with CaDiCaL and a separate four-vertex-cell lower bound. The per-model results are in wide_summary.json.
* The single_open.py experiment required inclusion on one axis only, with a second axis allowed any column set except the original (which has the previously certified 191 ceiling). All 512 vertices remained available. With an 18,000-conflict budget, 24 of 72 cases were UNSAT and 48 unresolved.

A selected vertex must have one or two selected neighbors. The two endpoints have degree one. Connected components are recovered from the actual Q9 edge set. Any cyclic component is blocked and solving resumes. A returned path is verified again independently on every vertex pair. Per-axis occupancy counts permit doubly occupied columns. The strengthened MiniCard model counts occupancy of the 128 four-vertex squares formed by the selected pair of axes. The exact-cardinality variant restricts the parity split appropriately for 193 vertices. The variable-length variant retains only the parity lower bounds.

The positive controls use a source and another genuine 191-edge path. Both projected column sets must change, and part of the phase hint is removed. The partial and positive-only controls recovered 191 edges; both projections changed in two columns. In the positive-only test there were zero forced Q9 vertices and all 512 were eligible. Each returned witness was checked on 18,336 vertex pairs. Separate comparisons with all 65,535 nonempty vertex subsets of Q4 test 90 exact-size and 90 variable-size model cases.

Run audit.py for the exact-size summaries. Cases are reproducible from case(i), job(i), and the eight selected row numbers. Counts are test inputs, and individual records are kept for all conflict-limited runs. UNSAT counts are finite solver reports. These batches do not establish a global Q9 upper bound.

For the eight mothers and all 36 axis pairs each, the two variable-length solvers independently returned UNSAT on **288 of 288** inputs. MiniCard completed 252 immediately and the remaining 36 on retry. The CaDiCaL encoding with cell-count lower bounds completed 276 immediately and the remaining 12 on retry. Both allow unchanged projections, arbitrary extra columns, and any number of vertices at least 193. Thus a Q9 induced path of at least 192 edges can contain all the occupied columns of a given one of these eight mother paths on at most one coordinate axis in the tested coordinate frame. With just one required axis, the variable-length search ruled out 8 of 72 inputs; 64 reached budget. No proof traces were saved.

One traced relaxation produced 840 models, each with 31 cycle components of length eight. The static pruning adds all cycles obtained by traversing four different inner axes in sequence twice, within any four-dimensional face avoiding the two projection axes. The count is 35 axis choices times 32 face positions times 24 cycles inside Q4. verify_oct.py confirmed that the resulting 26,880 distinct sets each induce one connected 8-cycle. final_audit.py verifies the final per-case results, the independent CaDiCaL control path, and the coverage of all required coordinate pairs; audit.py checks the exact-cardinality tables and reference witnesses.
