# Q9 search with sixteen distinct Q5 layers

Among all rooted Q4 Hamilton orders modulo coordinate permutation and reversal, orders.py selects the 98 with at least two flips of each outer coordinate. The best independently checked witness from this experiment has **183 edges and 184 vertices**, in layer order 010203020102030 (transition coordinates). The complete coordinates are in best.json. The pairwise checker audit.py tested all 16,836 vertex pairs of this witness. The 192-edge target was not attained.

* half.cpp seeded actual four-layer square paths of at least 47 vertices at an interior position, then extended left and right respecting all adjacent-fibre column exclusions. The beam ran twice on each of the 98 orders with width 120. It completed 140,065 full candidates and reached 180 edges.
* eight.cpp reused true eight-layer fragments across different outer orders when their internal contacts remained legal. The first round ran 294 cases with width 256 and the next ran 980 with width 420. They completed 447,298 and 2,467,511 full candidates respectively, reaching 182 and 183 edges. Counts include repeated candidates. Both rounds saved at least one independently checked full witness for each of the 98 orders.
* repair.cpp released one to seven entire consecutive layers while fixing the other layers of a true 16-layer path. It made 126,000 interval-repair calls and visited 202,329,354 path-generation nodes, reaching 183 edges. The initial random-portal annealer coupled.cpp topped out at 128 edges over 4,900,000 proposals.
* make_half.py extracted 12,698 distinct oriented mask-and-port records for eight consecutive layers of at least 86 vertices. Of them, 618 met the necessary size threshold to have a partner taking the sum to 193. pair.cpp checked all 120 relative Q5 inner-coordinate permutations at the fixed crossing for every admissible pair from this finite catalogue. There were 1,578,960 placement tests across 38 of the 98 orders. For the remaining 60 orders no internally admissible pair in the catalogue met the size threshold. No placement was induced. The smallest overlap defect was seven chords on a 193-vertex path. audit_near.py independently expanded the masks and verified the seven chords from actual vertex pairs.
* warm.cpp ran 384,000 additional repairs from captured low-conflict 193-vertex layouts. Fixed-portal exact CSP subsequently tested 11,616 single-port replacement cases from the 22 saved low-chord states, including unchanged cases per state. All had an empty layer domain even before search. A distinct filter selected 261 layouts with no conflict between compulsory cross-layer ports. treecsp.cpp ruled out all 261 fixed portal assignments for 193 vertices. It also ruled out 193 vertices for the 124 saved distinct full true-path mask-and-port inputs of at least 180 edges, holding the 17 inner portal coordinates of each input fixed. These counts describe tested inputs and do not assert distinct tuples of portals. The same code recovered a true 184-vertex path as a positive control. The 185-vertex search on those 124 inputs exhausted 106 and hit the 60-branch budget on 18.
* The fixed-portal CSP enumerates every inner induced Q5 path from the specified entry to the specified exit. Its arc consistency tests require adjacent layers' masks to meet exactly at the shared port for consecutive visits or be disjoint for other contacts. An upper bound uses exact weighted dynamic programming on spanning trees of the outer adjacency graph, relaxing off-tree contacts. This bound is confined to the specified ports.
* The result files trials.json and near_summary.json distinguish positive paths, defect states, and bounded negative runs. These finite runs leave the 98 construction families open.
