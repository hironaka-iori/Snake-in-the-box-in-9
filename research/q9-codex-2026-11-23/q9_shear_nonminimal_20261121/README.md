# Detours in sheared Q9 snakes

The parent bank contains 131 verified 191-edge words. One facet is translated on any nonempty subset of the other eight coordinates. Retained original vertices occur in their original order. One or two complete stretched transitions may take an induced route two or four edges longer than their Hamming distance. Other complete stretched transitions and any retained boundary portions follow arbitrary geodesics.

For the earlier 332 residual windows, the first relaxation ignores both terminal portions and permits the entire interior of up to two chosen stretched transitions to disappear. All other complete transitions still have to admit mutually compatible geodesics. Every genuine detour solution on that core would survive after deleting its detours and terminal portions. Tests with exactly two omissions also cover one omission because another surviving gap can be deleted. The result for these fixed cores uses new relaxed SAT runs. No geodesic-only UNSAT status is used as a detour result.

The fresh scanner works separately on geodesic base lengths 190, 188, 186, and 184 to target 192 after gains of 2, 4, 6, or 8. Each affine placement is scanned with retained terminal subedges and without them. A full gap individually blocked by other anchors must be among the detours. The preliminary filter rejects a core with more than two such gaps. The retained core, endpoint masks, and required split length are recorded. Cases where the final 192-edge slice cuts inside one of the proposed nongeodesic detours itself lie outside this enumeration.

The native relaxation enumerates all admissible geodesics of a gap up to a cap. Overlarge domains are removed for free before the compatibility search, and up to two more domains can be skipped. Exhausting this more permissive problem excludes an actual detour completion. A positive relaxation or a search cutoff is inconclusive. The SAT relaxation uses selection gates for the omitted interiors. Certified-infeasible contiguous subwords also exclude larger cores carrying them. allrelax.log, routes*.log, trims.log, final94.log and strong.log preserve these runs. Several methods cover the same cases. The separate short run in hit.log was stopped after 13 expensive unknown cases when the combined methods reduced its original 326 planned cases to 94. None of its unknown cases is counted as an exclusion.

The direct positional model tests specified detour sites and lengths. An interior vertex at relative position t is restricted by distance from both endpoints and parity. Fixed anchors, rank-to-rank adjacency, global distinctness and nonconsecutive contacts are enforced. The two designated detours can use different excess lengths. All completed candidates must be induced and have precisely 193 vertices. The breadth and retry logs distinguish propagation contradictions, UNSAT, and budget-limited outcomes. No LRAT files are claimed for this batch.

Three true positive controls are recorded. Two on an earlier real short segment have both boundary portions nonempty. A separate native search chooses a real window with exactly one original crossing and checks detours of excess two or four. Starting from 192, it tries each length through the first success with integral endpoints. The alternate SAT implementation rebuilds this path and the two shorter controls from their anchor data.

From the repository root, run `python3 -B q9_shear_nonminimal_20261121/audit.py` to recompute the summary, lattice distances, source geometry, site counts and exact length of each reported path. The generators require a C++20 compiler and python-sat. The final results are local to the stated transformation family and anchors.

Postprocessing checks the final unresolved internal cores against the actual required terminal lengths. Their surviving 242 labelled windows reduce to 87 endpoint problems. Reinstating the boundary paths rules out 26 further labelled windows. The 216 remaining labelled windows reached the budget. In all of them the stretched transition flips all nine coordinates. A separate last40.log deepens the exact positional models remaining after the first independent retry. These exact models specify the detour positions and lengths, so an UNSAT model here excludes that specification only.
